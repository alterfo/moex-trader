# Recalculations for the referee report of 10 November 2026

Inputs are the published series, not new estimates: `artifacts/algopack-deflation/w5-base.csv`
(457 book dates, 450 IC dates, 3 January 2025 – 16 October 2026). The tool is `paper/recalc/main.go`,
standard library only.

```
go run ./paper/recalc artifacts/algopack-deflation/w5-base.csv
```

All figures below are reproduced output, not estimates written by hand. Every number that the
referee asked for and that does not require new data is here; the two that do (a borrow rate and
short availability recorded per date) are marked.

## 1. M3 — power of the forward confirmation rule

Rule as written in the paper: after 60 forward dates the 95% interval of the increment of the
composite IC over the baseline lies above zero. Data: daily increment series (450 dates), mean
0.0368, Newey–West SE (L = 10) 0.01466, t 2.51. Autocorrelation inflates the variance by 5.3×, so
450 dates carry an effective sample of 84.7.

| dates | SE of the mean | MDE (95% interval above zero) | power against 0.0368 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 60 | 0.0402 | 0.0787 | **14.8%** |
| 120 | 0.0284 | 0.0557 | 25.3% |
| 250 | 0.0197 | 0.0386 | 46.4% |
| 450 | 0.0147 | 0.0287 | 70.8% |

Dates needed for 80% power against the observed increment: 561 at `t > 1.96`, 1 056 at
`|t| >= 3.0`, 1 317 at `|t| >= 3.45`. A block bootstrap of the 60-date mean (10-date blocks,
4 000 draws) reproduces the SE: 0.0408 against the analytic 0.0402.

The referee's arithmetic holds: 15% power, MDE 0.079, "about 560 dates", "about 1 300 dates".
For m15 the precise MDE at 450 dates under the `|t| >= 3.45` rule is (3.45 + 0.8416)·0.01466 =
**0.0629**, so the published 0.07 should become 0.063.

## 2. m14 — the "about 2 300 trading days" of the equivalence calculation

The number is reproducible; only its inputs are missing from the text. One rule generates both
published figures:

```
T = ((z_0.975 + z_0.80) · sigma_LR / delta)^2 ,  z_0.975 + z_0.80 = 2.8016
```

where `sigma_LR` is the autocorrelation-inflated daily volatility of the book, not the plain one.

| delta, %/day | sigma = 0.766 (plain) | sigma = 0.941 (long-run, Bartlett) | paper |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0751 (detection) | 816 dates | 1 231 dates | about 1 160 |
| 0.056 (equivalence) | 1 467 dates | 2 215 dates | about 2 300 |

Inverting: 1 160 dates at delta 0.0751 implies sigma = 0.913; 2 300 dates at delta 0.056 implies
sigma = 0.959. Both sit inside the observed long-run band 0.941–1.048, so the published pair is
internally consistent to within 4–6% once the autocorrelated volatility is stated. The text should
print the formula and sigma_LR = 0.94 (Bartlett, unit weights) rather than a bare "about 2 300".

## 4. M5 — dividend compensation, bound

Bias per day = 0.96 · (yield gap between the long and the short leg) / 252. The gap, not the level,
is what survives the netting; the universe yield of 3.3% (median of 115 top-100 events, appendix B)
sets the scale.

| yield gap, pp | bias, %/day | share of the 0.0751 net |
|---|---|---|
| 0.5 | 0.0019 | 2.5% |
| 1.0 | 0.0038 | 5.1% |
| 2.0 | 0.0076 | 10.1% |
| 3.5 | 0.0133 | 17.8% |

One-sided worst case, if the long leg earned no dividend at all: 0.0126 %/day (17% of the net) at a
3.3% universe yield, 0.0191 at 5%. The referee's "0.01–0.02 pp/day" is the one-sided figure, not
the gap-adjusted one; the honest statement is a bound of 0.013 %/day, with a realistic 0.002–0.008
once the legs are matched.

## 5. M7 — sign tests under overlap

| test | result | p |
|---|---|---|
| months | 18 of 22 positive | 0.0022 |
| months, effective n | 15.4 (autocorrelations 0.049, −0.030, 0.206) | 0.0037 |
| months, break-even effective n | significance survives down to n = 10 | 0.055 |
| non-overlapping 10-date blocks | 38 of 45 positive | < 0.0001 |
| quarters | **7 of 8** positive | 0.035 |

The monthly sign test survives the overlap correction; it fails only if the effective sample falls
below 11 months, which the measured autocorrelation does not support. But the quarter count is
7 of 8, not 7 of 7: 2026Q4 (16 dates, to 16 October) has a mean IC of −0.266. The claim "positive
in all seven quarters" must become "six full quarters, and the partial fourth quarter negative".

## 6. Book statistics the text leaves implicit (m17) and one new number

Daily volatility 0.766%, daily Sharpe 0.0981, annualised 1.557 — the published 1.56 is the
independent-draws figure. Under autocorrelation the same series gives an annualised Sharpe of
**1.14** (paper's V' = 1.872) to **1.27** (Bartlett V' = 1.510). The paper should quote the
autocorrelation-consistent Sharpe next to 1.56; the referee asked for the volatility and the
factor, and the factor alone costs 0.3–0.4 of a Sharpe point. Skew −1.52 and raw kurtosis 16.37
reproduce exactly.

Sector-neutral book (m18): `artifacts/algopack-deflation/w5-verdict-sector.txt` gives DSR 0.399 at
N = 10 and 0.120 at N = 100 — the referee's "approximate" reproduction is exact in the repository.

Date accounting (m1): 457 book dates, 450 dates with a mature 10-day outcome; the gap of 7 is the
horizon plus the 20 October data cut.

## 7. Run on 10 November 2026: the two items that needed a rerun

**Row accounting (m13) is closed.** The matrix was rebuilt with the same code and flags
(`cmd/flowscan -mode matrix -lag 30`, 2020-01-03..2026-10-20): **163 610 rows, 1 644 dates**, one
date short of the published 163 709. All 163 610 rows fall on or after 2020-04-01, the first
training date, so nothing is lost before training starts. Rows with a mature 10-day forward return:
**159 726**, against the 159 708 the frozen model reports — a difference of 18 rows, a refresh
effect.

The 4 001-row gap is therefore not a filter defect: it is the rows whose 10-day outcome is missing —
the last ten dates (about 1 000 rows) plus scattered per-name gaps (about 2 900 rows spread over 943
dates). One sentence in the appendix replaces the open question.

**V1, read indicatively.** An independent scoring path from the frozen coefficients
(`artifacts/algopack-alphafit/alphafit-models-20261023.json`) applied to the rebuilt matrix
reproduces the A2 baseline (IC 0.0611 against the published 0.0589) but overshoots the ridge
composite (0.1158 against 0.0970), so the scores are not the published ones and the number is a
reading, not the answer: mean daily rank correlation of the composite score with the 256-day
momentum rank **0.26** over 449 dates (daily range −0.32 to +0.57).

Two things follow. The correlation is far below the 0.60-0.67 the paper reports for the TimesFM
forecasts, and it agrees with E1: 91 % of the composite's IC survives the removal of the momentum
span, so the composite is not a momentum proxy — the book-level tie in `docs/metrics/s8b-portfolio.md`
8.46 is a property of net returns and costs, not of the ranking content. The exact figure still
needs the forecast panel dumped by `cmd/alphafit`; the dump path is in the model zone and was not
touched here.
