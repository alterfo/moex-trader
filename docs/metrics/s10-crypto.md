## 10. Crypto transfer of the registered programme

Conventions specific to this section: one venue for prices (Binance USDⓈ-M perpetuals), retail fee tier, costs charged per name by its own trailing liquidity tier, funding charged from the venue's own settlement history, one market and one author. Money is in bps of book notional per day unless stated. Rank IC is measured over the 5-day forward close-to-close return; book P&L accrues one day at a time.

### 10.2 S1: break-even before any backtest

The cost model is frozen in `internal/cryptocost` and printed by `cmd/cryptofit -mode costs`. Retail tier, Binance, 2026-11-02. **The spread and impact columns are tier assumptions, not measurements**: no order-book data was downloaded in this stage (see 10.7), so each name is charged the value of its trailing-liquidity tier.

| tier (trailing median daily quote volume) | fee/side | spread+impact/side | all-in/side | round trip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| major >=1B | 5.00 | 1.50 | 6.50 | 13.00 |
| large >=100M | 5.00 | 3.50 | 8.50 | 17.00 |
| mid >=10M | 5.00 | 8.00 | 13.00 | 26.00 |
| small <10M | 5.00 | 20.00 | 25.00 | 50.00 |

Required gross per side per day = 2 x turnover x all-in cost. At the large tier this is 1.70 bps a day for a 10% daily turnover book, 5.61 for 33%, 17.00 for 100%. For comparison the MOEX programme charges 15 bps a side (30 bps round trip), so the same turnover costs half as much in crypto for large names and 1.7x as much for names below 10M a day.

Funding over the holding period, from an APR of 9%, 11% and 22%: 1 day 2.5/3.0/6.0 bps, 5 days 12.3/15.1/30.1, 10 days 24.7/30.1/60.3, 40 days 98.6/120.5/241.1. `cmd/cryptofit -mode s1` prints this arithmetic for all 46 rows at their pre-registered turnover; it is the table that closes rows before a backtest is run.

| lookback 10 | 13.44 | 2.69 | 1.50 |
| lookback 40 | 14.71 | 3.19 | 1.83 |
| top 50 universe | 17.43 | 2.43 | 1.36 |
| top 200 universe | 10.46 | 2.88 | 1.74 |

The sign is stable in every variant, the magnitude stays in 8-18 bps a day, and the break-even cost per side is 83.68 bps against 12.25 charged on average across the book (12.25 is the turnover-weighted average of the per-name tier costs in 10.2). Removing funding removes 39% of the net and takes t below 2: the cell is half an order-flow book and half a short-the-crowded-long carry, and the two are not separable at this horizon.

### 10.5 Placebo

Five within-date permutations of the signal through the identical book, costs and turnover: net +0.96, -4.91, -3.15, -4.78 and -1.13 bps a day (mean -2.60) against the real +13.71. The permutation reproduces none of the real net.

| C41 | reversal / gap family | MOEX lead failed on costs | 1-day reversal is significantly negative (t -3.05): the daily cross-section trends at one day, so there is no reversal to trade at this horizon in 2024-2026 | closed negative |
| C15 | funding carry | carry family, untested on MOEX equities | funding carry book net -23.56 bps a day: the crowded long pays, but the price move against the short leg costs more than the carry earns | closed negative |

Result of the first crypto stage: **0 candidates, 2 leads** (order flow, one-month momentum), 3 families closed negative, the remaining 41 rows untouched. No row is called confirmed: no cell reached the registered gate, no cell has a forward record, and no cell has a completed Reviewer pass.

What a lead is worth here: an order-flow book at 10% daily turnover earns a break-even of 83.68 bps a side against 12.25 charged, so the economics are not the obstacle for once. The obstacle is that the effect halves when carry is removed and that a 1033-day single-market window with t 2.89 against a 3.3 bar is, by the programme's own convention, not evidence of an edge.

### 10.7 What data this stage did and did not use

Used: `data.binance.vision` monthly `futures/um/klines` at interval 1d (open, high, low, close, base volume, quote volume, trade count, taker-buy base and quote volume) and monthly `futures/um/fundingRate` (settlement time, interval hours, rate), plus `fapi/v1/exchangeInfo` and `fapi/v1/ticker/24hr` for the contract classification and the liquidity order.

Not used, and this bounds every number above:

| fact | consequence here |
|---|---|
| no order-book snapshot or best-bid/offer history was downloaded | the "spread + impact" column of 10.2 is a tier assumption, not a measured spread; the break-even of 83.68 bps a side and the cost of 3.45 bps a day inherit that assumption |
| the C11 signal is built from the kline field `taker_buy_quote_volume / quote_volume` | it is an aggressive-flow share over 20 days, **not** the MOEX A2 book-imbalance signal; C11 is therefore transferred as a family member, not replicated |
| no open-interest or long/short ratio series | C33 and the crowding part of C15 are unread |
| no liquidation events | C41 was run as a plain one-day reversal and closed negative; the cascade form is untested |

Available and not yet pulled, all public and historical: `futures/um/daily/bookTicker` (every best-bid/ask update, about 130 MB a day for BTCUSDT, far smaller for mid-caps), `futures/um/daily/bookDepth` (one-minute snapshots of cumulative depth at 1% levels, about 0.5 MB a day for BTCUSDT, 34561 rows on 2026-10-01), `futures/um/daily/metrics` (30-minute open interest, top-trader and taker long/short ratios), `futures/um/monthly/aggTrades` and `premiumIndexKlines` (basis). The measured-spread read, the A2 replication on `bookDepth` and the cascade form of C41 all depend on that pull and are not part of this stage's numbers.
