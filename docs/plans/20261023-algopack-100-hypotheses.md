## Why a second AlgoPack plan

The first plan tested 12 order-flow hypotheses; one passed (A2) and A2 is under forward evaluation until about 2027-01-20. The same 146 million rows hold many unused columns (ticket size, order-to-trade ratio, aggressor prices, intraday profile, evening session, ticker-level `hi2`). This plan registers 100 hypotheses on them before any run. A split-debate (2 rounds, opencode as Skeptic) fixed the protocol; its settlements are binding here and are copied below.

## Protocol settled by the debate (binding)

1. **Verdict window 2025-01-01..2026-10-16** (about 420 dates, sub-halves split at 2025-12-31). Reason, verified in the files: all four datasets are regenerated by MOEX for earlier history (`SYSTIME` is years after the trade date: `tradestats` 2024-01-10 24622 of 24622 rows patched, 2025-01-14 none; `obstats` 2023-01-10 all patched, 2024-01-10 13340 of 28920 patched; `obstats` 2025-05 re-touches liquid names). 2020-01-03..2024-12-31 is exploration only (2023-07..2024-12 is a second exploration cohort that must show the same sign as a robustness read, never a verdict).
2. **Eligibility:** a date counts only if its `SYSTIME` is at most trade date + 30 days. One robustness run drops the bars patched in 2025-05. A provenance table per dataset, year and patch cohort is part of the report; year-by-year coefficients are a hard fail trigger: a Tier 1 row fails if its coefficient has the opposite sign with Newey-West |t| >= 2.0 in 2025 or in 2026 taken alone, whatever its pooled result.
3. **Verdict form.** This item applies to every Tier 1 row except where the row states its own criterion (H069 information-ratio criterion; H085-H088 conditional on H001). An h5 outcome is attributed to its start date when the sub-halves are split. Both parts are computed for every such row unconditionally (no filtering of the coefficient by the trading form, which would bias its t) and the verdict is their conjunction. (a) Net trading form: long the top quintile by the feature (sign fixed below) against the equal-weighted admitted universe, weights equal, 0.15% per side times turnover, net mean positive in both sub-halves. (b) Fama-MacBeth with Newey-West and the controls of item 11, |t| >= the frozen threshold of item 5. Cadence is the horizon: h1 rows rebalance daily; h5 rows are traded as five staggered sleeves, each held 5 days and rebalanced weekly on its own weekday, cost charged on each sleeve's turnover; H001 is the weekly case. Newey-West lags are max(5, h) with h the horizon in days. The key rate is context, not a criterion, for relative forms. One hedged F1-style form is run for the single best Tier 1 survivor.
4. **Tiers.** Tier 1 (28 rows, `[T1]`) are confirmatory: threshold frozen by item 5, 420-date floor per unit, verdict form as above. Tier 2 (72 rows, `[T2]`) are exploratory: no verdicts. One lead rule, fixed now: Benjamini-Hochberg q = 0.10 on the verdict window only, with the Newey-West p-values of item 3; the exploration window is reported descriptively and does not select. Survivors are called "leads" and become confirmatory only through a new plan that re-registers the row definition verbatim (no changed windows, signs or controls) with a new family size.
5. **Effective test count, computed not asserted.** Before any outcome is read, on the exploration window only, the cross-sectional rank correlation matrix of the 28 Tier 1 daily features (mean over dates) gives the effective number M_eff = 1 + (M - 1)(1 - Var(lambda) / M) (Nyholt, M = 28, lambda the eigenvalues). The Tier 1 threshold is max(3.0, the two-sided normal quantile at 0.05 / M_eff) and both numbers are written into this file before the first outcome is computed. Rows H085-H088 run only if H001 passes; their four holds (H085) and the survivor grid (H089) are judged per comparison at the two-sided level 0.05 / 4. Expectation written now so a wall of fails invites no re-tweaking: with about 420 dates, sub-halves of 240 and 180 and overlap from 5-day horizons, only an A2-scale effect (net edge near 0.05-0.08% a day) can pass, and a null outcome is an expected result.
6. **A2 variants** (windows, gates, terciles, de-meaning) are Tier 2, except H001 (weekly rebalance, net gate), the single A2-derived economic form in Tier 1. A2 itself stays under the forward rule of the first plan, which remains the primary claim.
7. Ex-dates from `data/dividends.jsonl`: days with an ex-date for the ticker, the day before and the day after are dropped from that ticker (read-only use of the file). Split days by the -45% / +80% rule of the first plan.
8. Everything else is inherited from the first plan: point-in-time universe (top 100 by 60-day median value), exclusion 2022-02-25..2022-03-28, bars 10:05..18:50 only, decision features from bars up to 18:30, rank scaling to [-0.5, 0.5], log abnormal return against the universe, bootstrap seed 20261022. Exceptions: H065, H066, H067, H068 and H083 use the evening or morning session (stated in the row), H060 decides at 10:05, H094 uses no returns.
9. No short book; 0.05% per side is descriptive; nothing touches the live path, config or models. A pass is a record for forward observation only.
10. No post-hoc doors: row definitions, signs, horizons, windows and the lists below are frozen with this file; any change after the first outcome is computed is a new plan.
11. **Controls per row.** The first plan's controls (returns of days t..t-5, log value turnover against its 20-day mean, mean `spread_lv10`) are used, except that a control whose rank correlation with the feature exceeds 0.7 (exploration window, outcome-free) is dropped for that row. The expected cases are written now: the `spread_lv10` control is dropped for H015-H024, the turnover control for H002, H028, H031 and H033-H035; the final per-row lists are written into this file with the M_eff numbers, before any outcome.

## Notation

`d` the decision day; `M5`, `M20`, `M60` trailing means over the previous 5, 20, 60 days including `d`; `z20` is (value - M20) / std over 20 days; `rk` cross-sectional rank. Columns: `tradestats` (T): `pr_open pr_high pr_low pr_close pr_std vol val trades pr_vwap pr_change trades_b trades_s val_b val_s vol_b vol_s disb pr_vwap_b pr_vwap_s`; `obstats` (O): `spread_bbo spread_lv10 spread_1mio levels_b levels_s vol_b vol_s val_b val_s imbalance_vol_bbo imbalance_val_bbo imbalance_vol imbalance_val vwap_b vwap_s vwap_b_1mio vwap_s_1mio`; `orderstats` (S): `put_orders_b/s put_val_b/s put_vol_b/s put_vwap_b/s cancel_orders_b/s cancel_val_b/s cancel_vol_b/s cancel_vwap_b/s` and their totals; `hi2` (H): `hhi_agressive(_buy/_sell) hhi_buy hhi_sell hhi_netflow_buy hhi_netflow_sell hhi_passive(_buy/_sell) hhi_volume` at 18:40. Outcome h1 is the abnormal return close(d) -> close(d+1); h5 close(d) -> close(d+5). A "day" feature is built from the joined bars of `d` up to 18:30 with the `tradestats` value weights unless stated. Sign `+` means a higher rank predicts a higher return.

Sign mechanisms, fixed before any run (the sign is not revisited after a result):

- H016 `-`: a narrow top-of-book spread against a wide 10-level spread means a thin book behind the touch; the price is a fragile quote, not liquidity.
- H019 `-`: a spread that widens into the close signals inventory dumping by market makers, which reverts the next day.
- H038 `-`: a close near the daily high on the 18:30 snapshot is a buying-pressure overshoot that reverts at daily horizon; the continuation reading is the competing hypothesis and is covered by H041.
- H047 `-`: a high order-to-trade ratio is quote churn by fast participants, not informed demand.
- H056 `-`: placement that is large against the standing book is short-lived liquidity and carries no direction.
- H076 `-`: a close at the top of the 20-day range is the short-term reversal side of the momentum literature at h5.
- H097 `-`: concentrated passive (resting) flow means few dominant liquidity providers; their withdrawal is priced in as risk.
- H098 `-`: concentrated volume is the attention-spike reversal case.
- H068 `+`: a wide evening spread against the day spread means thin evening liquidity and a larger information risk premium that the next session pays.
- H083 `+`: the thin evening session prices news from after the close; the next session continues it.

## Implementation clarifications (written 2026-10-23 before the first run, no outcome seen)

These settle points the rows left open; none depends on a result.

- Tool: `cmd/flowscan`, one Go program, rows H001-H100 as the table above. Modes: `prep` (outcome-free: M_eff, control collinearity, writes `docs/plans/20261023-algopack-100-hypotheses.frozen.json`), `explore` (2020-01-03..2024-12-31, halves split 2022-07-01, descriptive), `verdict` (feature dates 2025-01-01..2026-10-16, halves split 2026-01-01, SYSTIME rule on, threshold from the frozen file).
- History: days before 2025-01-01 are loaded without the SYSTIME rule and feed only rolling windows, the 60-day universe and controls; every decision date and outcome of a verdict run is from 2025-01-01 on and passes the rule at row level (a row whose `SYSTIME` is more than 30 days after the trade date is dropped).
- M for the Nyholt formula is 23: the 28 Tier 1 rows minus H069 and H085-H088, which derive from H001. M_eff and the threshold are computed by `prep` on the exploration window from the mean daily cross-sectional rank correlation matrix and written to the frozen file before any outcome is read; the same file holds the control lists of protocol item 11.
- Bars: main-session features use bars present in all three datasets up to 18:30 (at least 20), windows need at least 8 bars (17:00..18:30), 5 (10:05..11:30), 10 (halves), 3 (18:00..18:30 and 10:05..10:35); evening features need 20 bars of `tradestats` and `obstats`, morning 10. Closing price of the day is the last `tradestats` close in 10:05..18:50; the 18:30 price must come from a bar at or after 17:55.
- Dividends: the ex-date is the first trading day after `last_buy_date`; the day before, the ex-date and the day after are dropped for that ticker (its return, its feature row and any outcome span containing them).
- Trading form: top quintile by sign times feature, equal weights, abnormal return against the equal-weighted mean of the admitted universe, cost 0.15% per side on the sum of absolute weight changes against the portfolio held `h` trading days earlier (the same sleeve); the net and gross per day are the holding-period values divided by `h`. A date with no portfolio `h` days earlier is charged a full purchase.
- H012, H020, H099: rank(A2) times (rank(other) + 0.5), so the other variable scales A2 instead of flipping its sign. H044: mean of the two ranks when they have the same sign, 0 otherwise. H045: sell share of value in bars with a negative `pr_change`, at least 5 such bars. H010: A2 of the lowest `spread_lv10` tercile of the day, others missing. H011: A2 on days when (high - low) / close is below the ticker median of the previous 20 days.
- H069: IR is the annualised mean over standard deviation (times square root of 252) of the net daily series. H077: neighbours are the five names with the highest correlation of daily log returns over the previous 60 days. H078: rolling 60-day regression of the ticker A2 on the universe-mean A2, residual on day `d`. H079 and H081 use the signal times (beta - 1) in the cross-sectional regression, beta from the previous 60 days against the equal-weighted universe return. ## Errata of the first verdict run (2026-10-23)
