# What investment funds say they trade: 100 hypotheses on our data (2026-10-27)

Status: DONE 2026-10-27 (run once: 0 candidates, 1 lead, 0 of 10 placebos; English only); amended by the errata below after the Reviewer's twenty conditions and before the first outcome was read. Owner's request: go through investment funds, take the description of what their strategies rest on, and generate and test 100 more hypotheses with the Reviewer.

## What the funds say (sources read 2026-10-27, secondary and fund-marketing material, so indicative only)

- **AQR** (published material): four style families (value, momentum, carry, defensive/quality/low risk) and time-series momentum, long and short, across asset classes; "defensive" is defined by low beta, low volatility, profitability and earnings quality; momentum is typically the last 12 months of return.
- **Winton, Man AHL, trend followers in general**: systematic trend following across instruments, long and short; trend strategies lose in sideways markets.
- **Renaissance / statistical arbitrage houses (DE Shaw, Two Sigma are described only loosely)**: short-horizon mean reversion of residual returns, market- and industry-neutral baskets, signals that decay with size; the academic descriptions (Avellaneda-Lee, Da-Gurun-Warachka, Blitz et al.) say the reversal is concentrated in the residual after removing market and industry returns and conflicts with industry momentum.
- **Dimensional, Research Affiliates (fundamental indexing)**: size, value, profitability, with fundamentals-based weights.
- **Macro / risk-parity houses**: exposure by volatility targeting and by trend of the market; transmission of macro shocks to assets through measured sensitivities.
- **Russian funds** (T-Capital "Trend stocks" BPIF is a systematically rebalanced momentum basket of index names; Alfa-Capital's MOEX conservative strategy tilts to stable dividend payers and raises cash when the outlook is weak; Wermuth WEELS is a long/short/neutral quantitative model on liquid large caps).

## What our data can and cannot test

- **Cannot be tested on history: value, quality, profitability, fundamental weights.** Our fundamentals file is a snapshot (first date 2026-10-19), not point-in-time history. These families are recorded as "forward only" and are not part of the 100; the daily snapshot (`cmd/fundsnap`) is the forward log.
- **Can be tested:** everything built from the point-in-time top-100 matrix (daily open, high, low, close, turnover, 2020-04..2026-10), the macro and commodity series, the sector lists in `cmd/alphafit/neutral.go`, and for the interaction family the AlgoPack flow columns.

## Method (frozen, run once; the errata at the end define the exact rules)

- **Stage I, exploration grade (columns A-I, K).** Daily rank IC of the column against the 10-day abnormal return (`y10`) on 2021-04-08..2024-12-17, Newey-West lag 10, plug-in bandwidth and 60-day block bootstrap, halves split 2023-07-01. Support needs |IC| of at least 0.02, |t| of at least 2.0 and the same sign in both halves. The 2021-2024 history is not shown to be free of the exchange regeneration, so stage I never confirms.
- **Stage R, realtime window 2025-01-03..2026-10-16.** Incremental slope over the frozen out-of-fold composite plus `mom120` and `mom256` ranks (per-date OLS, rank-scaled; the `tfmb` baseline), halves split 2026-01-05, IC with the same sign. Hit: Newey-West lag-10 |t| of at least 3.452 (Nyholt-adjusted, errata 9), plug-in |t| of at least 3.0, 99% block-bootstrap interval excluding zero, same sign in both halves and for the IC.
- **Placebos.** Five within-date permutations of A01 and five time-shift placebos (A01 at 260 and 380 dates, A04 at 260, C04 at 380, D02 at 520). One placebo hit voids the run.
- **CANDIDATE** = stage R hit plus stage-I support with the same sign, in a family not excluded in advance and not redundant with a composite input. Everything else that hits stage R is a LEAD for the forward log. Nothing here is a result on its own.
- **Book rules (G).** On the frozen composite sleeve, both `net_gp25_blend` and `net_d_blend`: timing benefit against the same-duty constant-exposure book, NW t of at least 2.6, positive halves, above the 95th percentile of 200 block permutations at block lengths 20 and 60 (errata 15). Costs of changing exposure are not in the primary statistic, so a pass is an upper bound.
- **Economics.** Only a CANDIDATE proceeds to a sleeve test in a later plan with the real short carry 25-29% and the bbo cost model. Nothing here changes the live system.

## Honest expectations

Most of these families are well known; after composite and 12-month momentum are in the baseline, most columns should be absorbed (TimesFM and slow momentum were). Prior of at least one CANDIDATE: about 2-4% (errata 16). The most likely outcome is that reversal and low-risk columns show stage-I effects that the composite already contains. A null is reported as a null. The sample is one market and one author.

## Errata

Written 2026-10-27 after the Reviewer's pass, before the first outcome was read.

1. **Stage I is exploration grade, not independent.** The AlgoPack provenance record (`20261023-algopack-100-hypotheses.md`) calls 2020-01-03..2024-12-31 exploration only, and the price panel was not shown to be unpatched. Stage I therefore supports a column but cannot confirm it; stage R is the only confirmatory gate. The verdict word is CANDIDATE, never CONFIRMED.
2. **Peers (H01-H04).** The refresh key was wrong in the first draft (all-missing columns); fixed: peers are refreshed at every date index divisible by 20 from 260, from the last refresh at or before the date, using `ab` over the last 250 dates ending at the refresh with at least 150 co-observations, five names, at least four with a value. Measured: mean share of the five peers replaced at a refresh 0.244. H coverage is 897 stage-I and 457 stage-R dates (`fundcover.txt`).
3. **Missing returns are NaN, not zero.** Only dividend-drop days are neutralised (return and abnormal return 0, the project convention). Any window containing any other missing day is NaN. Unit tests cover it.
4. **All windows live on the panel date index**; a name is scored only when every date in the window is present and admitted; the 2022-02-25..2022-03-28 hole is inserted as empty weekdays so it invalidates every window across it. E01, E02, E07, E08 are anchored on the date index (d-250, d-500, d-260..d-240, d-521..d-501). Realised coverage is printed per column (`artifacts/algopack-fund-playbook/fundcover.txt`).
5. **Counts before the run:** 250-day columns have 669 stage-I dates (not 919) and 457 stage-R dates; E02/E03 start 2022-03-29, E08 2022-04-22; B03/H05/H06 widths are about 50 names (sector map covers 49.4% of rows, at least five names per bucket required); K03/K05 about 42; F04 (USDRUB) has only 20 stage-R dates, F10 440, E06 210.
6. **Stage I ends 2024-12-17**, the last date whose 10-day label closes before 2025-01-03; stage R loses the final ten dates to missing labels.
7. **Macro (F).** Each macro change is the change between real prints (new print since the previous trading date, dated at most the previous trading date, as in `internal/macro`); no forward-filled zeros; OLS beta of `ab` on the change from real-print pairs over 250 dates ending the day before, at least 150 pairs; shock = sum of the last five real changes within 30 dates, divided by the series' standard deviation times sqrt(5) (so the shock is a z-score, comparable across series; the first draft multiplied the beta by the same sd, which cancelled, and was corrected). USDRUB and CNYRUB are counted as one test. F is reported but excluded from CANDIDATE in advance (its IC flips with the common shock sign; the market-regime reading cannot be separated here), so every F hit is a LEAD.
8. **Excluded from CANDIDATE in advance (LEAD only):** F (above), E06 (about 45 month transitions, premium from past windows only), J (flow history is regenerated and has no stage I), K (algebraic blends, robustness), M256 (benchmark, not in the family). J stays in the 94 for multiplicity.
9. **Multiplicity.** Nyholt M_eff from the 94 columns' cross-sectional rank-correlation matrix on stage I, computed without any outcome: **M_eff 89.8**; family-wise z for 5% 3.452; **stage-R threshold |t| of 3.452** (`fundmeff.txt`, passed as `-thr 3.452`). The 3.0 floor is kept as the lower bound.
10. **Inference.** The pre-registered statistic is Newey-West lag 10. Because 40+ columns are slow trailing levels, a CANDIDATE must also have |t| of at least 3.0 under an automatic plug-in bandwidth (Andrews AR(1) rule) and a 99% 60-day block-bootstrap interval of the incremental slope excluding zero. The plug-in bandwidth and the bootstrap replace a per-column ACF table (no ACF is printed). IC and slope series are re-contiguated by dropping missing dates before the variance estimate, so the lag-10 variance does not see the real gaps; the block bootstrap is the check on that. M256 sits in the same table as the benchmark (IC-only rows); its quoted figures in erratum 12 (IC 0.038, t 2.56 on 919 dates) are from an earlier run, the printed row governs (fewer stage-I dates under the strict window policy).
11. **Floors.** Fewer than 200 testable stage-I dates, fewer than 100 testable stage-R dates, or fewer than 30 names on a date: NOT TESTED, excluded from the family size, never read as absence of edge.
12. **Effect-size floor and years.** Stage-I support needs |IC| of at least 0.02 (mom256's pooled IC is 0.038 at t 2.56 on the same dates), |t| of at least 2.0, the same sign in both halves; the number of calendar years with the same sign is printed.
13. **Redundancy map** (`fundcover.txt`): rank correlations with the 75 AlgoPack inputs plus controls, on stage-R dates. Pre-declared redundant (|rho| above 0.7): B14 (CLV +0.88), D01 and D05 (Sp1m/Amihud +0.95/+0.91), D02 (Depth +0.92), D07 (VwapDev +0.74). A null on these says nothing about the fund family. Columns at 0.4-0.7 (the volatility group against RV/RSup, B02/B06 against CLV and Skew, K02, K04) are partly absorbed by construction.
14. **Placebos.** Five within-date permutations of A01 (mechanism control) and five time-shift placebos covering the fast and slow families (A01 at 260 and 380 dates, A04 at 260, C04 at 380, D02 at 520) for false-positive calibration; one placebo hit voids the run.
15. **Book rules (G).** The exposure lag is one day by construction (a test asserts that changing today's return does not move today's flag). Primary comparator: the same-duty constant-exposure book (timing benefit = (e - mean e) x net). Pass: timing-benefit NW t of at least 2.6, positive in both halves, and the mean above the 95th percentile of 200 block-permutations of the flags at both block lengths 20 and 60. Reported: switches per year, drop-top-5-days mean, four half-year blocks and every calendar month, Sharpe and drawdown of the scaled book, cost-adjusted mean at an assumed 0.10% of capital per switch, on both `net_gp25_blend` and `net_d_blend` (the base sleeve is partial-adjustment smoothed). Power: only cuts are allowed, so a rule with duty tau gives up tau times the drift before any timing benefit; a failure says little about exposure rules as a class.
16. **What a null does not test.** A null at a 10-day horizon, on a top-100 liquidity universe, without point-in-time fundamentals, with a composite of IC 0.097 plus mom120 and mom256 in the baseline, does not test value, quality, profitability, size, the 1-5 day statistical-arbitrage form or the 12-month form; it tests only "an incremental 10-day cross-sectional column". Minimum detectable effect: the composite's IC standard error over about 450 dates is 0.0163 (IC 0.0957 at t 5.87), so a hit at |t| 3.45 with 80% power needs an incremental IC of about 0.07. Prior of at least one CANDIDATE: about 2-4%.
17. **Freeze.** SHA-256 of `cmd/alphafit/fundgen.go` and `cmd/alphafit/fundtest.go` and of the feature file are recorded below before the first outcome; the formulas are the code. **No post-hoc doors:** any change after the first output is a new plan with a new family size.
18. **Added after the Reviewer's re-check:** `-thr` has no default and the run stops when it is below 3.0; a stage-R hit on a column with |rho| above 0.7 against a composite input is a LEAD, never a CANDIDATE; the bootstrap interval is printed at both ends; peer lookup, macro real-print alignment, the 2022 hole and the E06 date selection each have a unit test. **Correlated hits count as one finding:** hits within one family, or with |rho| above 0.7 between their stage-R IC series, are one finding; the Nyholt number is built from cross-sectional correlations, not from the IC-series correlation that governs the joint null, so it is a floor on the bar, not the final word.
