# Does AlgoPack improve the forecasts? Incremental, non-return and horizon tests (2026-10-23)

Status: REGISTERED 2026-10-23 after one Reviewer pass (13 amends applied, see review log; English only). Roles: the Claude session proposes and writes code (Proposer, sole writer), the opencode pane reviews read-only (Reviewer). No outcome variable of any row below has been computed. The only AlgoPack results seen are `docs/metrics.md` 8.15 and 8.16.

## Why a third AlgoPack plan

Plans 20261022 and 20261023 asked one question: does a rank of an order-flow column predict the next-days abnormal return of a top-100 universe net of 0.15% per side? Answer: A2 (book imbalance) is statistically stable (t +19, positive in every year) and economically near zero in the realtime window; 0 of 28 confirmatory rows passed. They did not ask the question the owner asks: does AlgoPack improve the forecasts of the model that is actually deployed, and does it forecast things other than direction (volatility, spread) that the live loop can use. This plan asks exactly that, and also closes two cheap A2 economic variants (longer hold, wider universe).

## Gate 0: overlap audit (done before this plan was written)

Facts verified in the files on 2026-10-23: the deployed ensemble (`ensemble_model.json`, `scripts/daily-retrain-box.sh`) trains on 18 tickers (`T18`), 10-day absolute label with a 0.5% deadband, features `FEAT18`; `order_book_imbalance` is one of the 18 columns but is structurally 0 in every historical export (`docs/knowledge/feature-pipeline-and-inference.md`), so no order-book-imbalance information reaches the model; `volume_zscore_20d` is raw turnover only and no client-type or order-flow split exists in `FEAT18`. There is no redundancy by construction; the incremental tests below are meaningful.

## Protocol (binding for every family)

1. **Verdict window**: feature dates 2025-01-03..2026-10-16, the only realtime-captured window (provenance reasons of plan 20261023, protocol items 1-2; the `SYSTIME` at most trade date + 30 days rule applies at row level). Halves split 2026-01-05. 2020-01-03..2024-12-31 is used as training history and as a descriptive exploration read; it never gives a verdict.
2. **Reuse, no redefinition**: AlgoPack feature definitions are the ones of plans 20261022 and 20261023 (daily aggregates from joined bars up to 18:30, ranks scaled to [-0.5, 0.5] only where a row says rank), implemented by the same code (`cmd/flowscan` features). Nothing is tuned on the verdict window; no feature is added after this file is signed.
3. **No new door after a result**: row definitions, arms, folds, seeds, thresholds and signs are frozen with this file; a changed definition is a new plan. Errata are listed in a section below, with the reason, before a rerun.
4. **Compute**: heavy runs (training, the folds of family A, the full panel of families B-D) on the ai-box, after a check that ComfyUI and TTS are idle enough (the box showed 19 GB of 24 GB VRAM in use at 2026-10-23); the Mac runs only short single-process checks and the outcome-free preparation. Data crossing to the box is the daily aggregate files, not the 7.3 GB raw tree.
5. **Multiple testing**: families A-D carry their own thresholds below; there is no pooling across families and no promotion of a failed family because another passed.
6. **A pass is a record, not a deployment**: nothing enters the live path, config or model artifacts; a passing family leads to a separate plan (shadow challenger via `model.challenger_ensemble_path`) and an explicit owner decision.
7. **Seeds and noise**: any learned model is trained with five fixed seeds (20261023..20261027); the reported number is the mean over seeds of each fold's paired difference, and the verdict also requires the sign of the mean difference to agree in at least four of five seeds, for the pooled ΔAUC and the pooled ΔIC each separately; the A-lin and B-lin arms have no seed variation by construction (lesson of step 17 in `docs/knowledge/model-quality-history.md`: single-seed replays cannot rank recipes).

## Family A. Incremental value for the deployed model (the direct answer)

**Setup.** Rows: ticker-days of the 18 production tickers (`T18` of `scripts/daily-retrain-box.sh`) with the existing `exportdataset` recipe: label 10-day absolute, deadband 0.5%, features `FEAT18` exactly as production, including `news_sentiment`, `news_count` and `order_book_imbalance`; `order_book_imbalance` is structurally 0 in every export, while the news columns are backfilled non-zero wherever `data/news_history.jsonl` (155072 articles) has rows, identically in both arms, so any difference between arms is on the ten AlgoPack columns only. Trainer: the existing `scripts/export_ensemble.py` path (lgbm + xgb + logreg), unchanged hyperparameters, run through `ENSEMBLE_*` environment variables. `export_ensemble.py` writes only the model JSON and a fixed-window validation AUC; it is reused per fold unmodified except for a new `ENSEMBLE_SEED` environment variable (default 42; the script hardcodes `random_state=42` today and the logistic member is deterministic). The out-of-fold predictions, the paired delta-AUC and delta-IC and the bootstrap are computed by a new Go command `cmd/algopackincremental` that scores the per-fold model JSONs with the Go inference of `internal/model` (no new Python). Nothing else in the recipe changes. The training-history choice is fixed by the family E rule before any A outcome is computed. History from 2020-01-03 for both arms (the production recipe uses 2024 on; a 2024-start run is the robustness arm).

**Arms.**
- B (baseline): the deployed 28-name order, algopack block absent.
- A (treatment): B plus the AlgoPack block of exactly ten daily columns, defined in plans 20261022/20261023 and frozen here: A2 daily (`obstats` `imbalance_val`, value-weighted), A2 M5, A1 trade imbalance, A4 net order flow, A5 cancellation intensity, H003 level asymmetry, H015 log(`spread_lv10`/M20), H002 depth growth, H025 ticket-size z20, H047 order-to-trade z20. Cross-sectional ranks within the 18 names are NOT used (the model sees the raw daily aggregates standardised on the training fold only), because with 18 names a rank loses the level information a tree can use.
- A-lin (control, logistic only): the deployed StandardScaler plus LogisticRegression member trained on the full `FEAT18` plus the ten block columns; B-lin: the same logistic on `FEAT18` alone; the linear gain A-lin minus B-lin against the ensemble gain A minus B separates the linear from the nonlinear share; same folds and seeds.

**Folds.** Expanding window, quarterly test blocks B1 2025-01-03..03-31, B2 04-01..06-30, B3 07-01..09-30, B4 10-01..12-31, B5 2026-01-01..03-31, B6 04-01..06-30, B7 07-01..10-16 (the last block is 3.5 months). Embargo: the deployed `label_date` filter (`label_date` strictly before the block start minus `ENSEMBLE_EMBARGO_BARS` = 10 calendar days, conservative) or a feature-date cutoff of 10 trading days; a feature-date cutoff of 10 calendar days leaks about 4 trading days of labels and is forbidden. Five seeds, identical rows and identical fold edges for B and A.

**Statistics (paired by date, the arms share every row).**
- A1 (the verdict statistic): the pooled out-of-fold AUC on the deadband-filtered label; ΔAUC = AUC(A) - AUC(B) with a moving-block bootstrap by date (block 10 days, 5000 draws, seed 20261022).
- A2 (second statistic): the daily cross-sectional Spearman IC of the predicted probability with the 10-day forward return; ΔIC per date, Newey-West (10 lags) t of the mean.
- Realised replay: not part of the verdict (the replay sums are noise at this scale, step 17); reported once through `cmd/backtest` with the same signal source for context.

**Accept A (all required)**: ΔAUC point estimate at least +0.016 and the 95% bootstrap interval above 0 on the full verdict window; ΔIC mean positive with Newey-West t at least 3.0; both halves have ΔAUC > 0 and ΔIC > 0; the seed-agreement rule of protocol item 7; the placebo below passes. Anything smaller is "no measurable improvement"; the deployed recipe scores 0.50-0.52 on the validation split and 0.4905 (range 0.479-0.498) on 15 purged walk-forward configurations over 2025-04..2026-10, and step 5 of `docs/knowledge/model-quality-history.md` measured the same-test N95 at 0.016, so +0.016 is the smallest material gain.

**Placebo (the negative control, run with A)**: the same ten columns shuffled across ticker-days within the date (cross-sectional permutation, seed 20261022), arm A-shuf. The verdict is on the placebo-corrected difference: ΔAUC(A) minus ΔAUC(A-shuf) must have its 95% bootstrap interval above 0, and the halves, seed and +0.016 conditions apply to this corrected difference. A placebo arm whose own gain over B is positive and significant is reported and voids the pass regardless (harness gain by construction).

**Breadth read (descriptive, not a verdict)**: the same A/B comparison on the point-in-time top-100 universe (about 100 names, same label and folds) to measure what 18 names hide.

**Power note (written now).** About 457 dates of 18 names over 7 folds; the fold AUC standard error is near 0.01-0.015, the paired difference of the two arms smaller because they share rows; a true ΔAUC under +0.005 cannot be resolved; the paired same-rows design is expected to shrink the difference variance below the 0.016 single-configuration N95, and the report states the achieved minimum detectable ΔAUC from the bootstrap. A null result therefore means "no improvement of +0.01 or more at this sample", and the report states the minimum detectable ΔAUC from the bootstrap.

## Family B. Realised volatility forecast (non-return target)

Target: log realised volatility of 5-minute closes (`tradestats`) of day d+1 (B1) and the mean over d+1..d+5 (B2), 10:05..18:50 bars. Test: Fama-MacBeth by date over the top-100 universe, cross-sectional rank regression of the ranked target on the ranked features, controls: today's realised-volatility rank (mandatory, so the feature must beat the AR(1) persistence), 5-day and 20-day mean volatility ranks, log turnover rank. Features (one regression per feature, frozen list): A2 absolute value, depth growth H002, `spread_lv10` log/M20, cancellation intensity A5, order-to-trade z20 H047, ticket-size z20 H025, level asymmetry absolute H003. Statistic: Newey-West (max(5, h)) t of the controlled coefficient. Threshold: pooled Newey-West |t| >= 3.0 with the same sign in both halves (the precedent of plan 20261022; per-half significance is not required, a pooled t of 3.0 implies per-half t near 2.1), over the family of 14 tests (7 features, 2 horizons); Benjamini-Hochberg at q = 0.05 on the pooled Newey-West p-values inside the family. Added value read: the out-of-fold R-squared gain of a pooled ridge with the AlgoPack block over the same ridge without it (the fold date edges of A; the rows are the top-100 panel, not the 18 names), reported with the bootstrap interval. No cost hurdle: the use is position sizing, stops and execution timing, and a pass only registers a candidate for those uses.

## Family C. Relative spread forecast

Target: rank of `spread_lv10` mean of day d+1 within the date, with the day-d rank, the 20-day-mean rank and the log turnover rank as controls (persistence is the benchmark). Features: the same list as B. Statistic and thresholds as the amended B (pooled |t| >= 3.0, same sign in both halves, BH q = 0.05 inside the family of 7). Use: the live loop's limit-order placement and entry timing; a pass registers a candidate only.

## Family D. Two A2 economic variants

- D1: A2 daily rank signal held 10 days, rebalanced daily as ten staggered sleeves, trading form of plan 20261023 protocol item 3 (long top quintile minus the equal-weighted top-100 universe, 0.15% per side times sleeve turnover), Fama-MacBeth with Newey-West 10 lags and the controls of plan 20261022. Accept: the controlled Fama-MacBeth coefficient has Newey-West t >= +3.0 (the registered A2 sign), the net mean is positive in both halves, and the controls are those of the frozen file of plan 20261023 for A2.
- D2: A2 daily rank signal, the universe widened to the top 200 and to the top 300 by the 60-day median value, same trading form at h5 (five sleeves). Accept per universe: the controlled Fama-MacBeth coefficient has Newey-West t >= +3.0 (registered sign +) and the net mean is positive in both halves, as D1. Wider spreads can erase the edge; that is the test.

D is a family of 3 comparisons (D1, D2-200, D2-300); the threshold per comparison is t >= 3.0 with a net-positive condition in both halves; there is no further adjustment across the three comparisons (precedent: plan 20261022 used |t| >= 3.0 for a family of 12). A2 itself stays under the forward rule of plan 20261022 and nothing here pre-empts the reading about 2027-01-20.

## Family E. Regime and provenance diagnostic (descriptive, no verdict)

Decompose the first-half versus second-half gap of A2 into (i) regenerated 2020-2024 versus realtime 2025-2026 on matched ticker cohorts, (ii) key-rate regime, (iii) realised-volatility regime, (iv) breadth of A2 and the median `tradestats` ticket size as the retail-participation proxy (no client-type split exists in the four datasets; retail flow share is not measurable in the repo). Output: tables of the A2 gross coefficient and net per day by regime, with Newey-West intervals; the stated purpose is to decide whether the regenerated history may be used for training in family A (if regenerated and realtime A2 features differ in distribution beyond the H094 check, family A is rerun with realtime-only training as the primary arm; this decision rule is fixed now: a Kolmogorov-Smirnov distance above 0.10 between the patched and the realtime bars of the same trade dates (the H094 overlap of plan 20261023, 2023-2025, where both exist for the same ticker-day) for any of the ten block columns of the T18 tickers triggers it; the era-vs-era KS of item (i) is reported descriptively and does not trigger, because an era difference is confounded with regime change. If the rule triggers, the primary arm of family A trains on realtime-only history and its verdict folds start at the first block whose training window holds at least 60 realtime trading days (block 2025-07-01 on); the earlier blocks are reported from the 2020-plus arm, descriptive only).

## Deliberately excluded (with the reason)

- Meta-labeling on the ensemble signal: rejected already (`docs/knowledge/model-quality-history.md`, +0.038 pp per trade against +0.10 required) and the deployed AUC leaves no primary skill to label.
- A standalone GBM search on raw columns: the 112 earlier tests show only linear-in-rank structure; the nonlinear question is answered inside family A (arm A against A-lin).
- Event-window level-crossing tests: block C of plan 20261022 ran them with placebo controls (231610 real events); a new variant is a separate plan.
- Evening-session rows: realtime capture exists only from 2025; revisit in 2027.
- Execution-cost use of spread and depth as an alpha test: it is cost engineering and cannot rescue a zero-gross signal; family C only registers the forecastability of spread.

## Verdicts and the stop rule

Each family reports pass, fail or not testable by its own conjunction; a fail is final for those definitions. If every family fails, the result is the written statement: "AlgoPack adds no measurable forecast improvement of the kind and size registered, at this sample", with the minimum detectable effect of each family. If a family passes, the next step is its own confirmation plan with the definitions copied verbatim and a shadow run through the challenger mechanism; no live change without an owner decision. The honest prior is low for A and D (earlier direction tests failed after costs), higher for B and C (volatility and spread are known to carry order-flow information); a null outcome is recorded in the same way as a pass.

## Errata

Errata written 2026-10-23 before any outcome of this plan was computed (implementation facts that the registered text left open or that turned out not to exist):

1. Tool placement. The outcome-free block builder is the mode `export` of `cmd/flowscan` (it reuses the panel and the feature functions in place; `flowscan` is a `package main` and cannot be imported), not a new `cmd/algopackfeatures`. The paired scoring is the new Go command `cmd/algopackincremental` (subcommands `merge` and `score`).
2. Run order step 1 of E and the KS rule. The registered rule compares "patched and realtime bars of the same trade dates (the H094 overlap)". No such overlap exists in the repo: `H094` as executed only counts rows by `SYSTIME` cohort, and the four datasets hold one version of each bar. The rule is replaced, before any outcome, by the adjacent-window form: the Kolmogorov-Smirnov distance of each of the ten block columns, pooled over the T18 tickers, between the last six regenerated months (2024-07-01..2024-12-31) and the first six realtime months (2025-01-03..2025-06-30). A distance above 0.10 for any column triggers the realtime-only primary arm exactly as registered. The era comparison is descriptive otherwise.
3. Row set. Rows of the `exportdataset` file whose ten block columns are not all defined (the 20-day warm-up, missing bars) are dropped from every arm, so B, A and A-shuf share identical rows; the production row set is otherwise untouched (no dividend-day drop, labels as exported). Rows with an empty label (unmatured forward window) are not scored.
4. Placebo permutation. A-shuf permutes the whole ten-column vector jointly across the tickers of one date (seed 20261022), preserving the cross-column correlation, not each column separately.
5. Forward return for ΔIC. The 10-trading-day forward simple return comes from the `tradestats` last-session close of the `flowscan export` file (ten ticker rows ahead), skipping moves below -45% or above +80%; the label itself keeps its ISS source. A date enters ΔIC with at least 10 tickers having both a prediction and a forward return.
6. Linear arms. A-lin and B-lin are the logistic member of the A and B ensemble JSONs (the same StandardScaler plus LogisticRegression fit, scored through `MemberProbabilities`), not separately trained models; the logistic member is deterministic so it has no seed variation.
7. Seeds and trees. `ENSEMBLE_SEED` seeds both the xgboost and the lightgbm member (the script hardcoded 42 for both).
8. Embargo. `ENSEMBLE_TRAIN_TILL` and `ENSEMBLE_EMBARGO_SPLIT` are the block start date and `ENSEMBLE_EMBARGO_BARS` is 10 (calendar days on `label_date`), the conservative form of the registered rule; the export is made with `-split 2027-01-01` so that every row has `split = train` and the script's own date and label_date filters do the work.
9. Ticker renames. AlgoPack files carry `YNDX` before 2024-07 and `TCSG` before 2024-11 where the production list and ISS use `YDEX` and `T`; without a mapping `T` kept 466 of 1790 rows in arm A. The new modes `export`, `volfc`, `spreadfc` and `econ` of `cmd/flowscan` map `YNDX` to `YDEX` and `TCSG` to `T` at load time (the registered modes of plans 20261022 and 20261023 are not changed). Found by the row-coverage table of `merge`, before any model was trained.
10. E trigger fired. Step 3 of the run order, outcome-free, on 2026-10-23: the KS distance between 2024-07-01..2024-12-31 and 2025-01-03..2025-06-30 (T18 pooled) is 0.112 for `ap_a2`, 0.137 for `ap_a2_m5` and 0.140 for `ap_spread_ratio`, above 0.10 (the other seven columns are at most 0.073). The training set of the first primary fold holds about 100 trading days of 18 tickers; that is the registered cost of the rule and is stated in the power note.
