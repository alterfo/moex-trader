# AlgoPack futures and FX: 101 hypotheses on untouched data (2026-10-23)

Status: DONE 2026-10-23 (English only): Book 1 scored twice (before and after errata 1), Book 2 scored once, numbers in `docs/metrics.md` 8.18, results verified number-for-number by the Reviewer (corrections applied). Roles as in the earlier AlgoPack plans: the Claude session proposes and writes code (Proposer), the opencode pane reviews read-only (Reviewer). Step 0 (outcome-free data validation) is complete and its numbers are below; no outcome of any row has been computed. The owner asked for "another 100 hypotheses" after `docs/plans/20261023-algopack-round3.md`; this plan answers with 101 rows (75 testable now, 10 registered but deferred by data vintage, 16 exploratory), not forced to 100.

## Why this plan is different

Rounds 1-3 used the equity AlgoPack columns against equity outcomes of 2025-2026, a window that 112 earlier tests and three rounds have examined; another 100 variants of the same columns there would be noise-mining. The same AlgoPack key also opens the futures (`fo`) and currency (`fx`) markets: `tradestats`, `obstats` and `orderstats` with 5-minute bars from 2020-01-03 (probed 2026-10-23; `futoi` is not in AlgoPack, it is already covered by `docs/metrics/s8a-portfolio.md` 8.10). Nobody has tested these data, and the outcomes of futures-on-futures and FX-on-FX series are untouched by any earlier test. The plan therefore uses a two-book discipline agreed in the brainstorm with the Reviewer.

- **Book 1 (confirmatory-grade, outcomes untouched)**: every row whose outcome is a futures or FX series. Threshold from an outcome-free effective test count, halves, Benjamini-Hochberg, nothing enters the live path, a pass is a lead that gets a frozen forward rule.
- **Book 2 (exploratory, outcomes spent)**: rows whose outcome is an equity or IMOEX return. Those outcomes were examined by about 250 earlier tests of the program (a number recorded here, not a retroactive threshold); Book 2 rows are leads only (Benjamini-Hochberg q = 0.10), no verdicts, and a null is expected and uninformative about the predictors.
- 2020-2024 is not a confirmatory cohort anywhere: the history of all three datasets is regenerated by MOEX (see Step 0), so it is exploration and robustness-sign only.
- Global test counter, written down so the reuse is visible: equity-realtime outcomes about 250 tests before this plan; futures and FX outcomes 0 before this plan, 85 in Book 1 of this plan.

## Step 0 results (outcome-free, 2026-10-23, `cmd/futscan step0`)

- Download: `fo` and `fx`, three datasets each, 1774 day files per dataset per market, 0 failed requests, to the ai-box (`data/algopack-fo`, `data/algopack-fx`). `fo` has 206-215 contract groups by `asset_code` (CNY, Si, GOLD, NG, MIX, BR, ED, RTS, IMOEX, Eu, SILV, MXI, SBRF, PLT, single-stock futures and the perpetual-style `USDRUBTOM`, `CNYRUBTOM`, `GLDRUBTOM`, `EURRUBTOM`, `IMOEX` that have no expiry and no roll); `fx` has 21 instruments (the main spot ones: `USD000UTSTOM`, `CNYRUB_TOM`, `EUR_RUB__TOM`).
- **Provenance per dataset (the main Step 0 finding).** The share of rows with `SYSTIME` more than 30 days after the trade date, by month: `fo` `tradestats` and `obstats` are regenerated up to 2025-04 and realtime from 2025-05; `fo` `orderstats` is regenerated until 2026-07 and realtime only from 2026-08. `fx` `tradestats` is regenerated until 2026-01 (realtime from 2026-02), `fx` `obstats` and `orderstats` are realtime from 2025-07. The earlier equity datasets were realtime from 2025-01 for all three; futures and FX are not.
- Consequence, fixed now: a feature is computed only on days when every dataset it needs is realtime (late share at most 0.2), and so are its trailing baselines; an outcome needs the realtime `tradestats` closes at both ends. Testable dates in 2025-01-03..2026-10-16 (at least 20 liquid groups with the feature and the h1 outcome): BI 344, TI 345, DG 274, SR 274, TZ 275, but NOF 50, CI 50, OT 29 (they need `orderstats`). Rows on NOF, CI and OT are registered and **deferred** until 120 realtime dates accumulate (about 2027-02); they are not run on regenerated data for a verdict.
- Distribution shift: Kolmogorov-Smirnov distance between 2024-07-01..2024-12-31 and 2025-01-03..2025-06-30 for the `fo` features, pooled over groups, regenerated data allowed: BI 0.030, TI 0.012, NOF 0.017, CI 0.114, DG 0.034, SR 0.124, TZ 0.020, OT 0.035; the spread-based SR and the cancellation-based CI exceed the 0.10 line of the equity plans, which is one more reason for the realtime-only rule (nothing is trained here, so no regime choice is left open).
- Contracts: front contract of a group-day is the contract with the highest main-session traded value (at least 1 million RUB, at least 20 joined bars); 2860 front changes in 115649 group-days; no roll rule needs a calendar because every return is computed inside one contract (the close of the contract that was front on day d, at d+h), and a day is excluded when it is one of the last two trading days before the contract's scheduled last trading day, the 15th of the delivery month (parsed from the contract code, for example `SiH6` is March 2026) or the nearest preceding trading day; groups without a delivery month in the code are perpetual and get no exclusion. The scheduled date is known ex ante; the observed last trading date of a contract is only a Step 0 diagnostic. One-day absolute return quantiles of the front series: p50 0.0093, p99 0.0799, p99.9 0.1854, max 0.6165 (fo); p50 0.0047, p99 0.0556, max 0.2541 (fx). The outcome cap is therefore |return| at most 0.25 for h1 and 0.45 for h5, frozen here.
- Relative-spread persistence (rank correlation of day d and d+1, realtime, same contract): 0.998 (fo), 0.987 (fx): spread forecasting is closed for futures and FX by the same argument as for equities and is not a row here.
- Liquid universe: the top 30 groups by median daily value of the previous 60 days (at least 40 observations), recomputed every date; Fama-Macbeth needs at least 20 groups per date.

## Protocol (binding)

1. **Windows**: feature dates 2025-01-03..2026-10-16, filtered to the realtime-eligible days of each row (above). Halves: the first and second half of the row's own eligible dates, split at the median of its outcome-free eligible-date list: the dates on which the row's predictor (for FM rows at least 20 liquid groups, for interactions both components) is available and the group's `tradestats` of that day is realtime, computed without any return, cap, regression or outcome (`split` in `cmd/futscan`); the fixed calendar split of the equity plans would leave the FX rows with an empty first half, because `fx` `tradestats` is realtime only from 2026-02. Rows with fewer than 80 eligible dates are "not testable" and are never repaired.
2. **Excluded**: 2022-02-25..2022-03-28 (no effect on the window), the last two days of a contract by the scheduled-expiry rule of Step 0, rows without a front contract.
3. **Predictors** (daily aggregates from joined bars up to 18:30, main session bars 10:05..18:50): BI book imbalance (value-weighted mean of (`vol_b_l10` - `vol_s_l10`) / (`vol_b_l10` + `vol_s_l10`)); TI trade imbalance; NOF net order flow; CI cancellation intensity; DG depth growth log(depth / mean of the previous 20 days of the same contract); SR spread ratio log(relative spread / its previous-20-day mean); TZ ticket size z20; OT order-to-trade z20; aBI the absolute value of BI. Baselines use only days of the same front contract and only realtime-eligible days, at least 10. Signs are inherited from the equity plans (BI +, TI +, NOF +, CI +, DG +, SR +, TZ +, OT -), so no sign is chosen after seeing futures data.
4. **Outcomes**: the h-day simple return of the front contract of day d (close of d to close of d+h, main-session closes, one contract only), divided by the group's trailing 60-day standard deviation of daily returns times the square root of h (volatility-scaled), moves above the caps dropped; "abnormal" means minus the cross-sectional mean of the date. Volatility rows use the log of the 5-minute realised volatility.
5. **FM rows** (Fama-MacBeth by date over the liquid groups): the rank-scaled predictor and the controls (scaled returns of day d and d-1, scaled mean of d-2..d-5, log turnover z20) against the abnormal volatility-scaled return; Newey-West lags max(5, h). **Time-series rows**: the 60-day z-score of the predictor against the scaled return with the same three return controls, Newey-West (HAC) standard error with lags max(5, h). **Volatility rows**: ranked log realised volatility at d+1..d+h on the ranked predictor with the controls log RV(d), its 5-day and 20-day means, log turnover; two-sided; "material" only with an average cross-sectional R-squared gain of at least 0.01 (in-sample upper bound, reported).
6. **Threshold for Book 1**: `T` = max(3.0, the two-sided normal quantile at 0.05 / M_eff), M_eff the Nyholt number of the 12 distinct predictor definitions (8 predictors, |BI| and the three interactions) from the mean cross-sectional rank correlation matrix on the verdict window, outcome-free: `cmd/futscan prep` (interactions computed in their true rank-product form) gave M_eff 11.31 and `T` = 3.000; the threshold stays at the 3.0 floor for any M_eff below about 18.5. Benjamini-Hochberg at q = 0.05 over 75 rows is nearly redundant next to `T` = 3.0 (a two-sided p of 0.0027 clears it from rank 5 up), so a BH survival is never read as an extra hurdle; it only bites between |t| of about 2.6 and 3.0. The deferred rows are later judged under the same `T`, with Benjamini-Hochberg recomputed over all Book 1 rows testable at that time, in an addendum registered before they run. A Book 1 row passes statistically if its registered-sign (two-sided for volatility, dispersion rows) t is at least `T`, the half coefficients both have the registered sign and the pooled one, and it survives Benjamini-Hochberg q = 0.05 over the testable Book 1 rows. FM rows also report a tercile long-minus-short net on abnormal returns (the raw return minus the date's cross-sectional mean) of 4 times 0.03% per holding period (0.03% per side is an assumed futures cost, unmeasured; the stress is 0.10% per side), per day for h1 and per 5-day period for h5, and an "economic" flag needs it positive in both halves at both costs; the flag is reported only and is never promoted to a pass condition in any later plan without a new registration and a measured cost; the passes are statistical leads either way.
7. **Book 2**: Benjamini-Hochberg q = 0.10 over the Book 2 rows, registered sign required; leads only. Equity outcomes: stock closes from the equity `export` block, IMOEX closes from ISS (`data/macro/imoex.csv`), dividend days dropped (ex-date minus 1, ex-date, plus 1), abnormal against the equal-weighted top-100 mean.
8. **No new door after a result**: row definitions, signs, caps, universe size, cost and windows are frozen with this file; a changed definition is a new plan. Nothing enters the live path, config or models. A pass is a lead and gets a frozen forward rule written before any forward date exists; the daily job `scripts/algopack-daily.sh` (launchd `com.oleg.moex-trader.algopackdaily`, last four days refetched) now also fetches the `fo` and `fx` markets into `data/algopack-fo` and `data/algopack-fx` (`cmd/algopackfetch -market`), so that the forward data accrues from its first date; the days between 2026-10-21 and the first nightly run are covered by that refetch window.
9. **Honest expectation**: the equity-side effects in rounds 1-3 were at best economically thin; the prior of a material pass is low. A null outcome is recorded in the same way as a pass. Power: with 290-366 eligible dates (halves of 145-183) and 30 groups, a single FM coefficient needs a daily information coefficient of roughly 0.03-0.04 to reach t = 3.

## What this plan does not do

It does not test intraday scalping (owner decision 2026-09-17), global-pair FX flow to equities, spread-innovation forecasts (closed above), a standalone GBM search on raw futures bars (rounds 1-2 showed trees add nothing on these column families), or any row on the regenerated 2020-2024 history for a verdict. It does not touch the live path.

## Errata

1. **Expiry rule withdrawn (found after the first Book 1 score, before any rerun).** The Reviewer's pass replaced the observed-last-date exclusion by a scheduled rule (the 15th of the delivery month). The first scan showed that the rule is wrong for contracts with another expiry day: for `NG` it flagged the second half of every month as expired, so the predictor existed on 212 of 452 dates and its 60-day z-score on none (`cmd/futscan dbg fo NG BI`). Both the observed-last-date rule and the scheduled-15th rule are therefore removed: no explicit expiry exclusion remains. What protects the series is unchanged and ex ante: the front contract is the highest same-day traded value, so volume leaves a dying contract before its last days, and a window that crosses an expiry has no close at its end and is dropped. Stated openly: Book 1 was scored once with the scheduled-15th rule (1 of 85 statistically pass: E03) before this erratum, and is scored a second time with the rule removed; both outputs are kept (`artifacts/algopack-futures-fx/scan-book1-first.txt`, `scan-book1.txt`), the second is the registered one, and any row whose statistical status differs between them is reported as fragile.
