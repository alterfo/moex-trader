# Closing the gaps of an external backtest-audit checklist (2026-10-27)

Status: registered before the run. English only. Diagnostics of the existing composite (frozen hyperparameters, the verdict window 2025-01-03..2026-10-16, bbo costs, partial-adjustment rate 0.5, borrow 17% a year); no new hypothesis, no retuning, nothing enters the live path.

## Why

An external checklist for independent backtest verification (purged splits, point-in-time pools, data preprocessing, multiple-testing corrections, placebo and robustness tests) was compared with the programme. Five items were open: no noise test, no total-loss test for names that left the board, no total-return treatment of dividend gaps in the book, only two independent test periods reported, and no explicit stale-signal (shift) test of the composite. The minimum backtest length was covered by the deflated-Sharpe table of the deflation study and is only restated.

## Tests (all on the frozen composite, `cmd/alphafit -mode robust`)

1. **Noise.** Gaussian noise with standard deviation m x 0.289 (m = 0.25, 0.5, 1.0; 0.289 is the standard deviation of a rank-scaled column) added to every non-missing rank-scaled input, including the three controls, in training and test alike; models refitted; three seeds per level. Pass: at m = 0.25 the mean IC keeps at least 70% of the clean IC, and at m = 1.0 the mean IC is positive for all three seeds.
2. **Stale signal (shift).** The prediction of day d-k is scored against the outcome of day d for k = 0, 1, 2, 5, 10, matched by ticker; the partial-adjustment book is also run on the one-day-stale signal. Pass: IC at k = 1 is at least 70% of IC at k = 0 and the IC falls monotonically in k. A collapse at k = 1 would signal dependence on the very latest bar (look-ahead or microstructure artefact).
3. **Total loss.** Names whose last trading day in the raw 5-minute files is before 2026-10-10 are treated as delisted: a long position held at the end of the last trading day loses 100%, a short position is closed at no gain. Pass: the sign of the mean net per day of the partial-adjustment book does not change, and the loss is reported with the number of events that hit a long position. The economic gate of this composite already failed, so this test can only worsen it; the question is by how much.
4. **Dividends.** Amended before the first valid run: the book return of a ticker-day is already missing when the span reaches the day before, the day of or the day after an ex-date from the dividend calendar, so no dividend gap enters the book from the calendar; the calendar covers only 8 tickers in 2025-2026 (47 payments), so the remaining payers are not covered. Test: count the flagged ticker-days, and as a proxy bound set missing every next-day return more than 5 points away from the universe mean (dividend gaps of the large payers and news jumps alike), rerun the book. Pass: the mean net per day of the partial-adjustment book moves by less than 0.01 percentage points.
5. **Independent periods.** Per quarter (7 blocks) and per calendar month: mean IC, Newey-West t and the net of the partial-adjustment book. Pass: IC positive in at least 6 of 7 quarters and at least 80% of months.
6. **Minimum length.** The expected maximum daily Sharpe of N skill-free strategies (Bailey and Lopez de Prado, the false-strategy theorem) and the years needed for the observed annualised Sharpe to exceed it, for N = 10, 50, 100, 300, 800.

Costs, windows and seeds are fixed by this plan. One run, no reruns after seeing the numbers.

## Result (2026-10-27)

Numbers in `artifacts/algopack-audit/robust.txt`; the matrix was rebuilt from the raw files (163709 rows) and the verdict reproduced line by line (IC blend +0.0957, NW t +5.87; partial-adjustment book +0.075% a day, annualised Sharpe +1.56). A first run of the mode had a defect in the dividend step (zero payments applied because the matrix carries no close column) and a missing argument in the last print; both were fixed and the whole mode rerun once, with no change to any other number.

1. **Noise: PASS.** IC keeps 98% (m = 0.25), 93% (0.5) and 85% (1.0) of the clean value, positive for every seed (lowest +0.078). The partial-adjustment net falls from +0.075% to +0.073%, +0.061% and +0.035% a day.
2. **Stale signal: PASS on IC, fragile on the book.** IC +0.0957 / +0.0806 / +0.0756 / +0.0650 / +0.0519 at k = 0 / 1 / 2 / 5 / 10 (84% at k = 1, monotone decay: no sign of look-ahead). The partial-adjustment book on a one-day-stale signal earns +0.022% a day and on a two-day-stale signal -0.034%: the net edge does not survive a delay of one or two days, the rank information does.
3. **Total loss: PASS.** Seven names left the board before 2026-10-10 (AMEZ, CIAN, FIXP, INGR, LNZL, LNZLP, QIWI); two long positions across the four books were lost in full. Partial-adjustment net +0.072% against +0.075% (difference -0.003 pp, bootstrap [-0.010, +0.000]); the sign does not change.
4. **Dividends: PASS as a bound, not as a measurement.** 132 of 45636 ticker-days (0.29%) are within one day of a calendar ex-date and their next-day returns are excluded from the book. With every next-day return more than 5 points from the universe mean removed (4177 ticker-days over all years) the partial-adjustment net is +0.069% against +0.075% (difference -0.007 pp, bootstrap [-0.056, +0.047]). The dividend calendar covers 8 tickers in the window, so a full ex-date calendar remains the open data item.
5. **Independent periods: PASS, with weak ends.** IC positive in 7 of 7 quarters (NW t +1.09, +6.92, +5.06, +1.85, +7.31, +7.61, +0.26) and in 18 of 22 months; the net of the partial-adjustment book is positive in 5 of 7 quarters and 14 of 22 months. The first and the last quarter carry little IC (+0.035, +0.012).
6. **Minimum length.** The expected maximum annualised Sharpe of skill-free strategies over 1.81 years is 1.17 / 1.69 / 1.88 / 2.15 / 2.37 for N = 10 / 50 / 100 / 300 / 800; the observed 1.56 needs 1.02 / 2.14 / 2.64 / 3.46 / 4.20 years. With 50 or more trials the window is too short for the book's Sharpe to exceed the best skill-free result, in line with the deflated Sharpe probability 0.35 at 100 trials. The IC finding is not affected by this: it was tested against placebos, not selected as a best of N.
