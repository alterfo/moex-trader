# News, events and incident detection

Read before touching `internal/ingestion/news`, `cmd/newsfetch`, `cmd/trainnewsmodel`, `internal/features/events.go`, `internal/features/incident.go`, or the news gate in `cmd/trader`.

Moved verbatim from AGENTS.md on 2026-10-09; the rules in AGENTS.md itself still take precedence.

## GDELT — tried and abandoned, don't reintroduce

- Fully removed (`cmd/gdeltnews`, `internal/ingestion/gdelt`, related flags in `trainmodel`/`calibrate`) after repeated instability: geo-blocked without a SOCKS proxy, a strict 1-request/5s rate limit, and still sporadic 429s needing 55-90s backoff even through the proxy. Historical news now goes through `internal/ingestion/news` (RSS) instead. Don't re-add a GDELT dependency without solving the rate-limit problem first.
- `Kasymkhan/RussianFinancialNews` (HuggingFace; mirrored on Kaggle as `kkhubiev/russian-financial-news`) is a candidate historical news dataset, but it only covers 2009-06-19..2024-12-16 — it does not overlap the current training window (2024-09..present) and is not a substitute for live news history.

## Paid news API/channel — investigated and rejected (2026-09-28)

- Triggered by "should we pay for a news feed to fix RSS instability/coverage." Cross-verified by two independent agents (this session + an `opencode` session in the neighboring agterm pane) researching in parallel, plus live probes against every candidate. Verdict: **nothing to pay for.**
- **Root cause of the instability was a code bug, not a rate-limit/coverage problem** — see the `User-Agent` bullet in `docs/knowledge/validation-and-ops.md`. Fixing it is free.
- **The trading loop caps how much news latency can matter anyway.** `cmd/trader` runs its whole decision cycle on `poll_interval: 5m` (`config.yaml`), and the news-veto gate (`newsGateSignalSource.Generate`) only gets evaluated once per cycle — sub-minute news delivery buys nothing until that cadence itself is shortened. The bigger latency ceiling is free MOEX ISS quotes lagging **~15 min** for stocks (`docs/plans/20260927-moex-pulse.md`) — two orders of magnitude bigger than any RSS-vs-paid-API gap. Don't relitigate "pay for lower news latency" without first shortening `poll_interval` and moving off delayed quotes.
- **Licensed RU wires (Интерфакс СКАН/IFX, AK&M, ТАСС Б2Б, `commerce.1prime.ru`) don't publish self-serve pricing and gate onboarding behind a signed договор with a legal entity + a расчётный счёт** — not accessible to an individual regardless of budget. AK&M's own order form has no field for a physical person.
- **T-Invest API has no news endpoint** — confirmed against the live gRPC gateway (`NewsService/GetNews` → 404 on every host/token tried) and by grepping the full Finam Trade API REST reference (39 endpoints, zero `news`/`feed` hits). Neither broker integration already in this repo can supply news for free.
- **Foreign news APIs don't reliably cover RU/MOEX names.** Marketaux indexes 14 of our 17 tickers as entities (`TICKER.ME`) but its live `countries=ru` query returned `found: 0` articles; Event Registry's RU coverage is unverified (endpoints 401 without payment). NewsAgent Data ($29+/mo, the only vendor with a published self-serve price) turned out to be an aggregator of the same public RSS/Telegram sources we already poll, with no per-ticker filter field, and its free tier returned zero verifiable RU articles.
- **Free sources added instead:** `iss.moex.com/iss/sitenews` (exchange operational notices, no auth, `SourceTypeMOEXSiteNews` in `internal/ingestion/news`), `cbr.ru/rss/RssPress` + `/RssNews` (Bank of Russia — the only source covering the regulator, whose rate decisions drive SBER/VTBR/T/POSI directly), and per-ticker `news.google.com/rss/search` feeds (`GoogleNewsSources`/`AllSources` in `internal/ingestion/news/sources.go`, query = primary alias from `DefaultAliases()` + "акции"/"цена"/"курс") are all wired into `news.AllSources(cfg.Tickers)`, used by both `cmd/trader` and `cmd/newsfetch`. Google already dedupes the same wire-copy story arriving from 4-5 of the hardcoded RSS feeds — it's additive coverage, not a dedup mechanism for the existing sources. `disclosure.1prime.ru` (ЦБ-accredited issuer material-facts server, free to read, keyed by ИНН/ОГРН not ticker) was verified live but **not yet implemented** — no RSS/API, needs a bespoke scraper plus an ИНН→ticker map (buildable free from `iss.moex.com/iss/securities.json`); pick this up as a follow-up task, not a quick fix.
- **ToS decision TAKEN by the user on 2026-09-29 (supersedes "flag, not yet acted on"):** Telegram's Content Licensing terms prohibit using platform data to train/fine-tune ML models, and Google News RSS's feed copyright notice restricts use to "rendering results within a personal feed reader for personal, non-commercial use". Presented both explicitly (Telegram 2 channels MarketTwits/Банкста, plus 17 per-ticker Google News feeds added 2026-09-29) as a gate with an RSS+MOEX+ЦБ-only alternative; **the user chose to include Telegram and Google News in the training set, knowingly accepting the exposure.** The archive records `source` and `trust_weight` per record (`cmd/newsfetch` `record` struct), so a source-filtered rebuild remains possible later if the decision is reversed. Everything below about the sentiment classifier's numbers is therefore measured on a set containing ToS-restricted sources — do not present those numbers as if the sources were clean, and do not silently continue the backfill without re-confirming this decision.
- The same decision applies to the *price* model's `-news-history` backfill path, not only to `cmd/trainnewsmodel`.
- **Sentiment classifier is DEAD and retraining cannot fix it (2026-09-29, second larger attempt).** The deployed `news_classifier.json` scores 92% of headlines inside ±0.1 (range [−0.247, +0.266] over 800 unique archive headlines, only 7.8% exceed |0.1|) — `news_sentiment` contributes ~nothing to the live feature vector, and its val acc 0.529 is below the 0.552 majority baseline. Rebuilding the archive from scratch (9596 articles: RSS/Google/ЦБ/MOEX `-since-days 30` + Telegram `-retro-from 2026-04-01`) and retraining produced **four** variants, all below baseline accuracy (val AUC 0.493–0.540, accuracy 0.469–0.538 vs baseline 0.511–0.580) with a 0.31–0.46 train→val AUC gap. The freshly trained models are *alive* (~64% of scores exceed |0.1|) while the deployed one is dead — so deploying any variant would replace a harmless near-zero feature with an actively wrong one. Kept the 2026-09-16 artifact. Full table and the behavioural scoring run are in `docs/metrics.md` ("News classifier rebuild from a fresh 9596-article backfill").
- **The archive cannot be rebuilt to the 2026-09-16 depth (17x sample loss).** 9596 articles yield only 2855 train samples: `BuildNewsLabels` drops tickers whose `source.History` fails (`internal/model/newsclassifier.go:246`) and truncates the tail where `exitIdx >= len(candles)`. The 2026-09-16 classifier had 48294 train samples vs 2855 today, because Telegram retro reaches only 2 channels and RSS/Google expose no history API. Don't re-attempt a one-off backfill retrain — it is the documented data-scarcity bottleneck, not a tuning problem. Retry only after months of live `cmd/newsfetch` appends build a direction-label-balanced corpus.
- `cmd/trainnewsmodel` does **not** filter by configured tickers — it trains on every ticker in the archive. A fresh backfill is dominated by non-config names (VKCO alone was 41.5% of 9596 records, plus 8.5% FX tickers that the model must not trade at all), which is why filtering to the 17 config tickers *hurt* (val AUC 0.493, 759 train samples). Pre-filter the archive to `config.tickers` before training, and keep FX (`GLDRUB_TOM`/`SLVRUB_TOM`/`CNYRUB_TOM`) out of it entirely.
- Google News RSS needs `?hl=ru&gl=RU&ceid=RU:RU` and returns a redirect (`302`) before the real feed — Go's `http.Client` follows this automatically, no special handling needed, but don't strip query params when building the URL or the redirect target loses `ceid` case-normalization.

## Incident detector (2026-10-09)

- `features.DetectIncident`/`CountIncidentSources` (`internal/features/incident.go`) flag physical incidents (drone/missile strike on a facility, fire/explosion at a facility, work stoppage of a facility) in headlines of the last 48h, counted as distinct sources into `FeatureContext.IncidentCount`. It is NOT in `ToVector`/`defaultFeatureOrder` on purpose (sparse event features dilute `colsample_bytree`, see the feature-subset rule).
- `newsGateSignalSource` blocks BUY (HOLD, reason `incident`) when `IncidentCount >= news.incident_min_sources` (default 2) and sends a Telegram alert with a 6h per-ticker cooldown; SELL is never blocked. Keys: `news.incident_veto_enabled`, `news.incident_min_sources`. A running `./trader` needs a rebuild to pick this up.
- Go regexp `\w` is ASCII-only: Cyrillic suffixes need `[а-яё]*`. The first draft of this detector with `\w*` missed 12 of 13 YDEX Sasovo headlines. Known false-positive source: tickers with broad aliases (VKCO) receive generic news, hence the 2-source minimum.
- Source independence (2026-10-09): `CountIncidentSources` collapses publisher families (`Google News: *`, `Ведомости*`, `ЦБ РФ: *`) into one source via `incidentSourceFamily`, so three Ведомости sections no longer satisfy `incident_min_sources: 2` alone. Telegram channels (MarketTwits, Банкста) stay distinct but mostly repost the same wires; a per-wire dedup and a headline-burst alert were NOT built because there are only 12 `incident` events (needs >= 30 live triggers to set a threshold). A running `./trader` needs a rebuild.

## Causal event study (2026-10-09)

- **Causal event study of news topics run (2026-10-09), DiD/TWFE/AIPW, no effect found.** `cmd/eventstudy` on 17 tickers x 140 days: `sanctions` (71 events) and `negotiations` (58) show no market-adjusted effect on CAR[0,+1] or CAR[+1,+5] (stacked DiD and AIPW CIs include 0, MDE ~1%); `incident` is directionally negative (-2.3%) but N=12 is underpowered (Holm p 0.078). IV was deliberately not run (no valid instrument). Protocol `docs/plans/20261009-event-causal-study.md`, numbers in `docs/metrics.md`. Do not reopen without a news archive several times larger.
