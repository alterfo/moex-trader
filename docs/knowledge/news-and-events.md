# News, events and incident detection

Read before touching `internal/ingestion/news`, `cmd/newsfetch`, `cmd/trainnewsmodel`, `internal/features/events.go`, `internal/features/incident.go`, or the news gate in `cmd/trader`.

Moved verbatim from AGENTS.md on 2026-10-09; the rules in AGENTS.md itself still take precedence.

## GDELT — tried and abandoned, don't reintroduce

- Fully removed (`cmd/gdeltnews`, `internal/ingestion/gdelt`, related flags in `trainmodel`/`calibrate`) after repeated instability: geo-blocked without a SOCKS proxy, a strict 1-request/5s rate limit, and still sporadic 429s needing 55-90s backoff even through the proxy. Historical news now goes through `internal/ingestion/news` (RSS) instead. Don't re-add a GDELT dependency without solving the rate-limit problem first.
- `Kasymkhan/RussianFinancialNews` (HuggingFace; mirrored on Kaggle as `kkhubiev/russian-financial-news`) is a candidate historical news dataset, but it only covers 2009-06-19..2024-12-16 — it does not overlap the current training window (2024-09..present) and is not a substitute for live news history.

## Paid news API/channel — investigated and rejected (2026-09-28)

- Triggered by "should we pay for a news feed to fix RSS instability/coverage." Cross-verified by two independent agents (this session + an `opencode` session in the neighboring agterm pane) researching in parallel, plus live probes against every candidate. Verdict: **nothing to pay for.**
- **Root cause of the instability was a code bug, not a rate-limit/coverage problem** — see the `User-Agent` bullet in `docs/knowledge/validation-and-ops.md`. Fixing it is free.
- **The trading loop caps how much news latency can matter anyway.** `cmd/trader` runs its whole decision cycle on `poll_interval: 5m` (`config.yaml`), and the news-veto gate (`newsGateSignalSource.Generate`) only gets evaluated once per cycle — sub-minute news delivery buys nothing until that cadence itself is shortened. The bigger latency ceiling is free MOEX ISS quotes lagging **~15 min** for stocks (`docs/plans/20260927-moex-pulse.md`) — two orders of magnitude bigger than any RSS-vs-paid-API gap. Don't relitigate "pay for lower news latency" without first shortening `poll_interval` and moving off delayed quotes.
- **Licensed RU wires (Интерфакс СКАН/IFX, AK&M, ТАСС Б2Б, `commerce.1prime.ru`) don't publish self-serve pricing and gate onboarding behind a signed договор with a legal entity + a расчётный счёт** — not accessible to an individual regardless of budget. AK&M's own order form has no field for a physical person.
- **T-Invest API has no news endpoint** — confirmed against the live gRPC gateway (`NewsService/GetNews` → 404 on every host/token tried) and by grepping the full Finam Trade API REST reference (39 endpoints, zero `news`/`feed` hits). Neither broker integration already in this repo can supply news for free.
- **Foreign news APIs don't reliably cover RU/MOEX names.** Marketaux indexes 14 of our 17 tickers as entities (`TICKER.ME`) but its live `countries=ru` query returned `found: 0` articles; Event Registry's RU coverage is unverified (endpoints 401 without payment). NewsAgent Data ($29+/mo, the only vendor with a published self-serve price) turned out to be an aggregator of the same public RSS/Telegram sources we already poll, with no per-ticker filter field, and its free tier returned zero verifiable RU articles.
- **Free sources added instead:** `iss.moex.com/iss/sitenews` (exchange operational notices, no auth, `SourceTypeMOEXSiteNews` in `internal/ingestion/news`), `cbr.ru/rss/RssPress` + `/RssNews` (Bank of Russia — the only source covering the regulator, whose rate decisions drive SBER/VTBR/T/POSI directly), and per-ticker `news.google.com/rss/search` feeds (`GoogleNewsSources`/`AllSources` in `internal/ingestion/news/sources.go`, query = primary alias from `DefaultAliases()` + "акции"/"цена"/"курс") are all wired into `news.AllSources(cfg.Tickers)`, used by both `cmd/trader` and `cmd/newsfetch`. Google already dedupes the same wire-copy story arriving from 4-5 of the hardcoded RSS feeds — it's additive coverage, not a dedup mechanism for the existing sources. `disclosure.1prime.ru` (ЦБ-accredited issuer material-facts server, free to read, keyed by ИНН/ОГРН not ticker) was verified live but **not yet implemented** — no RSS/API, needs a bespoke scraper plus an ИНН→ticker map (buildable free from `iss.moex.com/iss/securities.json`); pick this up as a follow-up task, not a quick fix.
- **ToS decision TAKEN by the user on 2026-09-29 (supersedes "flag, not yet acted on"):** Telegram's Content Licensing terms prohibit using platform data to train/fine-tune ML models, and Google News RSS's feed copyright notice restricts use to "rendering results within a personal feed reader for personal, non-commercial use". Presented both explicitly (Telegram 2 channels MarketTwits/Банкста, plus 17 per-ticker Google News feeds added 2026-09-29) as a gate with an RSS+MOEX+ЦБ-only alternative; **the user chose to include Telegram and Google News in the training set, knowingly accepting the exposure.** The archive records `source` and `trust_weight` per record (`cmd/newsfetch` `record` struct), so a source-filtered rebuild remains possible later if the decision is reversed. Everything below about the sentiment classifier's numbers is therefore measured on a set containing ToS-restricted sources — do not present those numbers as if the sources were clean, and do not silently continue the backfill without re-confirming this decision.
- The same decision applies to the *price* model's `-news-history` backfill path, not only to `cmd/trainnewsmodel`.
- **Sentiment classifier is DEAD and retraining cannot fix it (2026-09-29, second larger attempt).** The deployed `news_classifier.json` scores 92% of headlines inside ±0.1 (range [−0.247, +0.266] over 800 unique archive headlines, only 7.8% exceed |0.1|) — `news_sentiment` contributes ~nothing to the live feature vector, and its val acc 0.529 is below the 0.552 majority baseline. Rebuilding the archive from scratch (9596 articles: RSS/Google/ЦБ/MOEX `-since-days 30` + Telegram `-retro-from 2026-04-01`) and retraining produced **four** variants, all below baseline accuracy (val AUC 0.493–0.540, accuracy 0.469–0.538 vs baseline 0.511–0.580) with a 0.31–0.46 train→val AUC gap. The freshly trained models are *alive* (~64% of scores exceed |0.1|) while the deployed one is dead — so deploying any variant would replace a harmless near-zero feature with an actively wrong one. Kept the 2026-09-16 artifact. Full table and the behavioural scoring run are in `docs/metrics.md` ("News classifier rebuild from a fresh 9596-article backfill").
- **The archive cannot be rebuilt to the 2026-09-16 depth (17x sample loss).** 9596 articles yield only 2855 train samples: `BuildNewsLabels` drops tickers whose `source.History` fails (`internal/model/newsclassifier.go:246`) and truncates the tail where `exitIdx >= len(candles)`. The 2026-09-16 classifier had 48294 train samples vs 2855 today, because Telegram retro reaches only 2 channels and RSS/Google expose no history API. Don't re-attempt a one-off backfill retrain — it is the documented data-scarcity bottleneck, not a tuning problem. Retry only after months of live `cmd/newsfetch` appends build a direction-label-balanced corpus.
- `cmd/trainnewsmodel` does **not** filter by configured tickers — it trains on every ticker in the archive. A fresh backfill is dominated by non-config names (VKCO alone was 41.5% of 9596 records, plus 8.5% FX tickers that the model must not trade at all), which is why filtering to the 17 config tickers *hurt* (val AUC 0.493, 759 train samples). Pre-filter the archive to `config.tickers` before training, and keep FX (`GLDRUB_TOM`/`SLVRUB_TOM`/`CNYRUB_TOM`) out of it entirely.
- Google News RSS needs `?hl=ru&gl=RU&ceid=RU:RU` and returns a redirect (`302`) before the real feed — Go's `http.Client` follows this automatically, no special handling needed, but don't strip query params when building the URL or the redirect target loses `ceid` case-normalization.

## Incident detector (2026-10-09)

- `features.DetectIncident`/`CountIncidentSources` (`internal/features/incident.go`) flag physical incidents (drone/missile strike on a facility, fire/explosion at a facility, work stoppage of a facility) in headlines of the last 48h, counted as distinct sources into `FeatureContext.IncidentCount`. It is NOT in `ToVector`/`defaultFeatureOrder` on purpose (sparse event features dilute `colsample_bytree`, see the feature-subset rule).
- `newsGateSignalSource` blocks BUY (HOLD, reason `incident`) when `IncidentCount >= news.incident_min_sources` (default 2) and sends a Telegram alert with a 6h per-ticker cooldown; SELL is never blocked. Keys: `news.incident_veto_enabled`, `news.incident_min_sources`. A running `./trader` needs a rebuild to pick this up.
- Go regexp `\w` is ASCII-only: Cyrillic suffixes need `[а-яё]*`. The first draft of this detector with `\w*` missed 12 of 13 YDEX Sasovo headlines. Known false-positive source: tickers with broad aliases (VKCO) receive generic news, hence the 2-source minimum.
- Source independence (2026-10-09): `CountIncidentSources` collapses publisher families (`Google News: *`, `Ведомости*`, `ЦБ РФ: *`) into one source via `incidentSourceFamily`, so three Ведомости sections no longer satisfy `incident_min_sources: 2` alone. Telegram channels (MarketTwits, Банкста) stay distinct but mostly repost the same wires; a per-wire dedup and a headline-burst alert were NOT built because there are only 12 `incident` events (needs >= 30 live triggers to set a threshold). A running `./trader` needs a rebuild.
- Incident short (2026-10-10, on top of the veto): with `news.incident_short_enabled: true`, an active incident (>= `incident_min_sources` sources in 48h) turns a BUY or HOLD into a SELL sized like the model's (same `TargetLotsForNotional` from `risk.target_notional`/`max_lots`/`vol_scale`, per `feature.LotSize`); the price model's existing SELL is left untouched. The risk gate caps it (`max_net_short` was 90000 in config.sandbox.yaml) and the executor skips once the position reaches the target, so repeated 5-minute SELL signals are idempotent. The short is news-opened only — the preflight gate cannot see news (it replays model-only), so incident shorts are not backtested; the position exits naturally after the 48h incident window expires and the model no longer yields SELL. Counted by the `incident_shorts_total{ticker}` Prometheus counter. Config keys: `news.incident_short_enabled`. A running `./trader` needs a rebuild+restart to pick this up.

- News-position exit (2026-10-10): with `news.exit_enabled: true`, a short opened by the incident gate is tracked (`cmd/trader/newsexit.go`, state in `news.exit_state_path`, default `artifacts/news_trades.json`) and closed by a `Flat` signal (BUY/SELL with `TargetLots=0`, `Flat=true`; `TargetPositionExecutor` and the orchestrator treat it as desired position 0, so it can never flip the position). Exit triggers: `stop_loss` (adverse move >= `exit_stop_loss_pct`), `take_profit` (peak gain >= `exit_take_profit_pct` and a retrace of `exit_trail_pct` from the peak, i.e. the news impulse has faded and the market corrects), `time_exit` (`exit_max_hold`). The exit signal repeats every poll until the position is flat; afterwards a per-ticker `exit_cooldown` (default 48h = incident window) blocks a new incident short, otherwise the same incident would reopen the short right after take-profit. Only positions the manager opened are ever closed: a short that existed before the incident (model book) is not tracked and is left alone. Defaults (1.5% / 0.7% / 2.5% / 24h) are NOT tuned: they are scaled from the observed incident CAR[0,+1] of about -2.3% on N=12 and need re-fitting once >= 30 live incident triggers exist. Not backtested (the preflight replay cannot see news). Tracker state lives in `audit_events` (stage `news_trade_state`, id `news-trade-state`), so it travels with the synced `trader-sandbox.db` on failover; `exit_state_path` is only a fallback for runs without a store. Follow-ups: `docs/plans/20261010-news-exit-tuning-and-buy-side.md`.

## Causal event study (2026-10-09)

- **Causal event study of news topics run (2026-10-09), DiD/TWFE/AIPW, no effect found.** `cmd/eventstudy` on 17 tickers x 140 days: `sanctions` (71 events) and `negotiations` (58) show no market-adjusted effect on CAR[0,+1] or CAR[+1,+5] (stacked DiD and AIPW CIs include 0, MDE ~1%); `incident` is directionally negative (-2.3%) but N=12 is underpowered (Holm p 0.078). IV was deliberately not run (no valid instrument). Protocol `docs/plans/completed/20261009-event-causal-study.md`, numbers in `docs/metrics.md`. Do not reopen without a news archive several times larger.

- Ticker matching and industry mapping (2026-10-11): `Matcher` used to do a raw substring match, so the VKCO alias `вк` matched inside «по**вк**ам» and the whole 2026-10-09 diesel-sanctions story (Trump–Putin call, ~25 headlines) was filed under VKCO/MOEX and none of it reached ROSN/LKOH/TATN. Aliases of <= 4 runes now need whole-word boundaries (`containsAlias`); longer ones must start at a word boundary so inflections still match. `news.industry_matching: true` (default off) adds `IndustryRule`s (`DefaultIndustryRules`): a headline with an industry keyword (e.g. «дизел», «нефтепродукт») is also attached to the sector tickers as `MatchedArticle.Indirect` with half the source trust. `CountIncidentSources` skips indirect matches on purpose: an industry-wide story like «санкции сняты» is direction-blind to the incident detector and would open an incident SHORT on the very tickers it helps. Turning it on shifts `news_*`/`event_*` feature values for oil tickers vs. what the model was trained on, so replay the preflight gate before enabling it live. There is still no BUY path from news (see `docs/plans/20261010-news-exit-tuning-and-buy-side.md` §3).

Incident shorts fire once per episode (2026-10-14): the 48h window made `incident-short` re-fire every 5-minute cycle (1000+ log lines for YDEX). `SummarizeIncidents` gives the start of the latest episode (12h gap clustering) and the latest article time; `newsExitManager.claimIncident` persists a per-ticker watermark and allows a short only when the episode starts after it and is at most 6h old, so follow-up coverage and a restart do not re-short. BUY veto still holds for the whole window.
Look-back filter (2026-10-14): `news.lookback_move_pct` (default 3, 0 disables) skips a news-driven entry when the 5-day move already went the same way (short after a fall of at least that many percent, buy after a rise). The episode is still consumed, so a skipped first report is not retried on later cycles. Only the incident short uses it today; `movePriced` is the hook for a future news BUY.
Denial rule (2026-10-14): an article in the window that mentions the strike but says normal operation, no damage or drones shot down or not reaching the target (`DetectIncidentDenial`, SBER Balakovo data-center case) sets `IncidentDenied`. A denied incident opens no short, and a tracked news short is closed (`forceExit`, reason denied) when the denial shows up later. The BUY veto still applies.
LLM veto for incident shorts (2026-10-14, owner-approved exception to the 2026-09-15 "no LLM on the trading path" decision): `internal/incidentcheck` asks qwen3.8 on ai-box (`news.incident_check_url`) once per incident episode whether the headlines show real damage. A short opens only on `damage`; `no_damage`/`unclear` consume the episode without a trade; a checker error or timeout opens nothing, restores the episode watermark and backs off 10 minutes, so the first report is retried while it is still fresh (6h). The LLM can only cancel a short, never create one or touch BUY/SELL from the model. Empty `incident_check_url` disables it. Live probe: SBER Balakovo headlines -> no_damage, YDEX fire/stop -> damage.

`news.lookback_vol_mult` (2026-10-15, default 0 = off): the look-back limit becomes max(`lookback_move_pct`, mult x daily realized volatility x sqrt(5)), so a 4% move is priced in for a calm bank but ordinary noise for a volatile ticker. The multiplier is NOT calibrated: no event study has been run on it, so it stays off in config until one is pre-registered. Idea source: GARCH-style volatility clustering (Simonov & Akhunyanova 2019 was read and rejected as a signal source: its p-adic forecasts are statistically insignificant on 7 instruments and untested out of sample).

## LLM in the risk contour: rules and gaps (2026-10-18)

Rules that already hold in code (do not loosen without the owner):
- The LLM never creates a position and never touches the model's BUY/SELL: `internal/incidentcheck` can only confirm (`damage`) or cancel an incident short; `no_damage`, `unclear`, empty headline list, error or timeout all mean no trade (`cmd/trader/incidentcheck.go`). The deterministic gates (risk gate, kill switch, `max_net_short`, news veto) run after it and cannot be overridden by it.
- Failure is closed: an unreachable checker opens nothing and backs off 10 minutes; an empty `news.incident_check_url` disables the path entirely and reverts to the deterministic detector.
- Exception scope: owner-approved 2026-10-14 for incident shorts only; any other use of an LLM on the trading path needs a new approval (2026-09-15 decision).

Gaps found on 2026-10-18 (not fixed, they touch the trader path and need a decision):
- Audit: a verdict is written only to the text log as `ticker, verdict, reason`. The headlines sent, the model name and version, the raw response and the prompt hash are not persisted, so a decision cannot be reconstructed after the fact. Proposed: one JSONL record per check (UTC time, ticker, model, prompt hash, headlines, raw content, parsed verdict, outcome) next to `news.exit_state_path`, with a regression test that a record exists for every outcome including error.
- Reproducibility: the model is addressed by tag (`qwen3.8:latest`), so a pull silently changes behaviour. Proposed: pin the digest in config and log it with each record.
- No quality monitor: the share of `damage` verdicts and their later price outcome are not tracked, so drift of the checker cannot be seen. H5 and H12 in `docs/plans/20261015-hypotheses-from-llm-review.md` cover the checks to run on the verdict log once it exists.

Localization status (what is and is not Russian-specific): Cyrillic-aware incident and topic detectors (`[а-яё]*`, not `\w`), Russian sources (RBC, Vedomosti, Interfax, CBR, MOEX notices), exchange calendar and CBR key-rate series. Measured limit: a bag-of-words headline classifier trained on 18375 Russian samples (2021-2024) shows no directional signal on 2026 (`docs/metrics.md`, "Data depth, step 2"); zero-shot LLM direction on Russian headlines (H2) has not been run. Localization is therefore done for detection and filtering, not for sentiment-as-alpha.

## Archive composition, measured 2026-10-18

`data/news_history.jsonl`: 14799 ticker-article pairs, 12706 unique articles. Sources: Telegram (MarketTwits 5575, Банкста 3679) 62.5% of pairs (64.8% since 2026-04), Google News 28.6%, everything else 8.9%; Telegram plus Google is 91.1%. By ticker: `VKCO` 28.5% (it was 41.5% of the older 9596-record snapshot cited above), SBER 7.8%, `MOEX` 7.2% (the exchange's own shares, not the index), YDEX 5.6%. There is no "market news without a ticker" class: broad-market headlines reach tickers through broad aliases, mainly `VKCO`, and the label is the ticker's excess return over IMOEX (`benchmarkTicker`), not the index reaction. ## Archive merge and ticker universe expansion (2026-10-18)

Why only 17-18 tickers: the matcher `news.DefaultAliases()` knew only the config tickers, so every importer produced pairs for them alone (the Kasymkhan dataset itself tags about 200 of the 260 TQBR shares). `news.Universe` (`internal/ingestion/news/universe.go`) now matches every ticker in `data/ticker_names.json` (ISS short, full and Latin names, `cmd/listtqbr -names-out`), the curated `DefaultAliases`, and aliases learned from the dataset's own `<name> <TICKER>` tags. It handles Russian case endings (stem plus a fixed suffix set, so `Магнита` matches and `магнитное` does not), multi-word names, `#TICKER` / `$TICKER`, and keeps a preferred/ordinary pair (`SBER`/`SBERP`) as one company. Aliases that map to several different companies are dropped.

Merged into `data/news_history.jsonl` (gitignored; snapshot of the previous 14799-pair file kept only in the session scratch directory) with `cmd/newsimport -names data/ticker_names.json -alias-blocklist data/news_alias_blocklist.json -parquet <train> -parquet <test> -jsonl data/news_history.jsonl -jsonl newssent/data/news_all.jsonl -jsonl newssent/data/news_backfill.jsonl -jsonl newssent/data/live/{news_merged,wires,news_live}.jsonl -jsonl <iss_sitenews converted>`. Old records are untouched (the new file starts with the old 14799 lines), duplicates are dropped by ticker plus article id and by ticker plus normalised title.

Result: 101082 ticker-article pairs, 70670 unique article ids, all 260 TQBR shares present (146 with at least 50 pairs, 84 with at least 200, 25 with at least 1000; the weakest are single-digit). Years 2021-2024: 6072 / 12175 / 24893 / 23874 pairs; 2025: 8785; 2026: 20528. The December 2024 - March 2026 hole is no longer empty (about 500-900 pairs a month: Telegram backfill and exchange notices), though still thin against 2023-2024. Sources: bcs 19.4%, smart_lab 14.9%, finam 12.6%, MOEX site notices 12.1%, MarketTwits 12.0%, bcs_tech 8.9%, Банкста 6.6%, rdv 3.6%, t_invest 2.9%; Telegram 18.7%, Google News 4.6% (before: 62.5% and 28.6%). `VKCO` fell from 28.5% to 4.5% of pairs by dilution, its own records are unchanged.

Quality check (against the dataset's own ticker tags as ground truth, `-audit`): title-only matching reaches 90.5% precision on 3995 hits (3617 correct) and recovers 3655 of 15514 tagged tickers (23%; most tagged articles name the company only in the body). Tags use retired tickers, mapped through `renamedTickers` (`YNDX->YDEX`, `TCSG->T`, `HHRU->HEAD`). The test suite for the matcher caught a real false positive (a generic first word, "мобильные", binding MTS) and the audit found others, now blocked in `data/news_alias_blocklist.json`: generic or colliding words (самолёт, пик, позитив, ренессанс, авиакомпания, элемент, кристалл, яковлев, дом, биржа), legacy wrong aliases (`SMLT` had Segezha's name) and FX-metal words. Alias precision on tagged titles overstates precision on general news, because tagged articles are finance-specific.

Known limits, so they are not rediscovered:
- BCS digests ("Ход торгов", "Старт дня") are tagged by BCS with every ticker they mention; they become market-commentary pairs for those tickers. Use `-max-tickers-per-article` in `trainnewsmodel` or filter by source.
- 12.1% of pairs are MOEX site notices (risk parameters, price corridors, listing notices): official and timestamped, but near-empty as sentiment text; filter by source `MOEX сайт-новости` for classifier training.
- Names that are ordinary words (Магнит, Полюс, Лента, Эталон, Астра) still leave some noise outside finance outlets.
- 97% of the Kasymkhan rows carry a date without a time, so intraday use is impossible for them; entry on the next trading day's open (`BuildNewsLabels`) avoids same-day lookahead.
- Licensing: the Hugging Face card of `Kasymkhan/RussianFinancialNews` says Apache 2.0, the Kaggle mirror used by `newssent` is recorded there as CC BY-SA 4.0; the underlying texts come from Finam, BCS, RBC, T-Invest and Smart-lab. Treat it as research data, keep attribution, do not redistribute the merged file. The earlier ToS decision on Telegram and Google News (2026-09-29) is unchanged and no new scraping was added.
- Results measured before this merge (news classifier steps 2 and 2b in `docs/metrics.md`) used the old 14799-pair archive plus the Kasymkhan import for the 18 config tickers; they are not recomputed here.

### Archive cleaning and reproducible rebuild (2026-10-18, owner approved)

The pre-merge archive held ticker bindings that its sources never supported (`newssent` measured about 43% retargeted rows; here 4211 of 4566 `VKCO` pairs were Telegram macro posts, Eurozone and Japan PMI releases were attached to `OZON` and `POSI`). `cmd/newsclean` moves such pairs into a quarantine file instead of deleting them, with a reason. A pair stays if the headline names its ticker under the current matcher, or it is a Google News feed for exactly that ticker, or it comes from a tag-backed source (bcs, bcs_tech, finam, smart_lab, rdv, t_invest, t_analytic) whose ticker came from the dataset's tags. Everything else goes to `data/news_quarantine.jsonl` (`not_supported_by_text` or `ticker_outside_universe`).

Result: `data/news_history.jsonl` is now 94175 pairs (65360 unique articles), 6916 pairs quarantined (4211 `VKCO`, 693 `GLDRUB_TOM`, 564 `MOEX` macro posts, 194 `CNYRUB_TOM`, the rest scattered; 6118 of them Telegram). All 260 TQBR shares still have news (143 with at least 50 pairs, 84 with at least 200, 24 with at least 1000). Years 2021-2026: 6063 / 12130 / 24887 / 23862 / 8698 / 13832. Sources: bcs 20.8%, smart_lab 16.0%, finam 13.5%, MOEX notices 12.8%, bcs_tech 9.5%, MarketTwits 9.0%, Банкста 4.5%, Telegram 13.6% in total, Google 4.8%. `VKCO` is 0.4% of pairs. The quarantined macro posts (global PMI, central banks, geopolitics) are real market-wide news and may become a separate "market" class later; they are kept, not discarded.

The cleaning run exposed a nondeterministic matcher: several aliases that share a stem (for example an inflected form of two companies) overwrote one another in map order, so two runs differed by about 40 pairs. Stem collisions across different companies are now ambiguous and dropped, aliases are processed in sorted order, and a test builds the universe 60 times. `scripts/rebuild-news-archive.sh` (needs `BASE`, `PARQUET_DIR`; optional `NEWSSENT`, `ISS_CONVERTED`, `OUT`, `QUARANTINE`) rebuilds the whole archive from the premerge file; two runs give identical files (md5). The premerge file (14799 pairs) and the pre-clean merged file (101082 pairs) are kept in `~/dev/archive/moex-trader-news/`.

The numbers in the previous section (101082 pairs, 70670 articles, 25 tickers at 1000+, Telegram 18.7%) describe the file before cleaning.

### More Telegram channels, `cmd/tgarchive` (2026-10-18, owner approved)
