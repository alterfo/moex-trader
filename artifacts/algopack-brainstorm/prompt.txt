You are a quantitative researcher brainstorming for a Moscow Exchange (MOEX) equity study. Answer in Russian.

DATA WE HAVE: MOEX AlgoPack "Super Candles", 5-minute bars 07:00-23:50, 2020-2026, ~450 tickers/day, four datasets: tradestats (OHLC, volume, value, trades, buy/sell split, disb = buy-sell imbalance), obstats (order book: spread at BBO / 10 levels / 1 mln RUB, depth, imbalance at BBO and full book, vwap of bid/ask side), orderstats (placed and cancelled orders by side, value, vwap), hi2 (11 concentration indices HHI of trades: aggressive, passive, buy, sell, net flow). Also futures and FX versions, 155k news headlines with tickers, dividends calendar, key rate, CNY, Brent, IMOEX, gold, RVI, OFZ indices, sector indices, futures positions of individuals vs legal entities (FUTOI). Only data since 2025-01 is real-time-honest (earlier history was regenerated by the exchange), about 450 trading days.

WHAT WE FOUND: single features give nothing. The A2 family (net order-flow imbalance, unexpected imbalance vs own 20-day history) predicts 10-day abnormal return (IC 0.06). A ridge+elastic-net composite of 75 daily features gives realtime IC 0.096, concentrated (79% of total IC) in days when the RVI volatility index is below its trailing median (IC 0.139 vs 0.045 when high). Short leg carries the spread. A daily-rebalanced long-short book loses everything to costs (0.15% per side); partial-adjustment execution nets about +7.5% a year at 17% borrow, 0 at 25%. Strongest A2 effect sits in thin stocks (t up to 7). Macro states (key rate, CNY, Brent, news counts) do not time anything; Saturn, Moon, Kp controls gave no false positives.

ALREADY TESTED (do not repeat): ~600 hypotheses including: imbalance, spread, depth, order cancel/placement ratios, VPIN, Kyle lambda, flow autocorrelation, time-of-day slices (early/late, halves, morning, evening), HHI on futures/FX, day-of-week, turn of month, macro regime states, sanctions news counts, lunar phase, Saturn, geomagnetic Kp.

NOT YET EXPLORED: horizons beyond 10 days; overnight vs intraday return decomposition and closing auction; real quoted spread as the cost in thin stocks; flow before dividend ex-dates, earnings and news bursts; lead-lag between large caps and followers; futures FUTOI positions crossed with equity flow; functional shape of the intraday profile; stock-level volatility regimes; cross-asset links (equity flow vs OFZ, vs futures basis); retail vs institutional behaviour proxies; options-like effects around quarterly expiries; index rebalancing days; ex-dividend capture; short-squeeze signatures from cancel/placement asymmetry.

TASK: propose 25 NEW, concrete, falsifiable hypotheses that a quant would not list first, ranked from most to least promising. For each: (1) one-line statement, (2) economic mechanism, (3) exact AlgoPack columns or other data to build the variable, (4) how to test it so that it can fail (statistic and what counts as failure), (5) why it might beat the costs. At least 8 must be unconventional (new mathematical models, cross-sectional networks, information-theoretic or topological measures, regime-switching, microstructure invariants). Be specific and short. No filler.
